## Review Focus

1. A grant arrives after12s while main is stalled or another host has won: the old utility cannot admit/tick/resume/commit (Tasks1,2,6).
2. Capture races reentrant input or paused commands: no pending ledger serialization, extra periodic rejection, ID reuse or gameplay trace drift (Task3).
3. Upload succeeds but its reply is lost, then Forget or a newer capture races: reconcile exact service bytes, retain the right committed/pending pair, never recreate cleared files (Task4).
4. A lost re-host claim reply or burned generation causes simultaneous contenders: one authority wins, others adopt the actual service generation, every restored run stays paused (Tasks5,6).
5. TURN acquisition overlaps early ICE, connection replacement or repeated failure: bounded buffering, no stale credentials, no quota-bypassing retry storm (Task7).
