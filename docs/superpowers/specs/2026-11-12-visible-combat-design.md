# Visible Combat and Sound (Demo-ii) Design

Status: drafted 2026-11-12 at the user's request ("write a spec, review, plan, review, implement subagent and review") from the three before-the-demo recommendations: a repaired console comes back powered, the enemy is drawn with its shield ring and weapon bar, and the ship makes sound. Parent: `2026-11-09-foundation-design.md` §7 (rendering, HUD), §9 (error handling); builds on Demo-i (`2026-11-11-demo-ready-design.md`), whose combat model (`enemy.maxHull/shieldLayers/shotArmTick/fleeing`, `enemyWeaponCharge`, `fleeSecondsLeft`) and HUD model this draws on.

## 1. Goal

Three things a newcomer hits in the first ten minutes, fixed: a console that was repaired but still does nothing (it came back unpowered and nothing said so); a combat they cannot see (the whole FTL layer is two text rows); and silence. After this feature a repaired console works again when the reactor has a bar to spare and the power panel says `OFF` when it does not, the enemy is a picture with a shield ring that empties layer by layer and a weapon bar that fills, and seven synthesized cues mark hazards, impacts, shots, the ready drive, the jump and the run's end.

**Out of scope (follow-ups):** an Engineering bot that re-powers consoles (the acceptance floor stays as Demo-i left it unless the repair change alone lifts it); beam tracers to a positioned enemy; recorded audio assets or music; a volume menu; a drawn sector map; new enemy templates.

## 2. Options considered

- **Unpowered after repair:** (a) `repairStation` returns a non-Engineering console at level 1 when `powerPool(state) >= 1`, else 0 **(recommended, chosen)**: FTL's behaviour, one line, closes the dead end; (b) only flag it on the HUD: the dead end stays; (c) reserve a bar per console: changes the whole power game.
- **Drawn enemy:** (a) a Pixi `Graphics` panel beside the threat panel, driven by a pure `HudModel.enemy` block **(chosen)**: no new art, every number already exists, testable model; (b) a sprite sheet: art the project does not have; (c) rows in the threat panel: that is what exists.
- **Sound:** (a) Web Audio oscillators synthesized in `app/audio.ts`, driven by a pure cue detector over counters the sim already keeps **(chosen)**: no assets, no licences, the detector is unit-tested, the synth is a thin sink; (b) recorded samples: sourcing and licensing work for a demo; (c) a library: a dependency for seven beeps.

## 3. Repaired consoles come back powered

`repairStation(state, i)` (`power.ts`): `failed = 0`, `repair = 0`, and for every kind except Engineering `level = powerPool(state) >= 1 ? 1 : 0` (Engineering keeps its frozen level as today). The pool is read **after** `failed` clears (a failed console holds level 0, so its bar is already in the pool). No draw. `shedPower` and the schema are unchanged. The golden replay includes repairs, so it is re-blessed once (cause recorded). The repair rule alone changes no state shape; the version bump to 17 comes from §4's new `lastHitTick` field.

HUD: `HudStation` and `EngineeringPanel.rows[]` gain `unpowered: boolean` = `!failed && level === 0 && kind !== Engineering && station present`. The power rows already print `OFF` in `WARN` for an offline console; the Engineering console rows now also print `OFF` (status) for an unpowered one, so the operator who must put the bar back sees it on their own panel. The controls card's line 4 becomes `Hold Interact at a fire, breach, failed console or downed friend. Repairs need a bar` (84 characters).

Bot: unchanged (Demo-i's rule: no seat at an offline console). With repairs returning powered, the Demo-i acceptance soak's four-player floor is retried at `nodesReached >= 3`; if the row still falls short the floor stays 2 and the figure is recorded.

## 4. The drawn enemy

`HudModel.enemy: { hull: number; layers: number; layersMax: number; weaponCharge: number; fleeSeconds: number; weaponsDown: boolean; enginesDown: boolean; shieldsDown: boolean; hitFlash: boolean } | null` (`null` while absent or without a state). `hull = enemy.hull / enemy.maxHull`; `layers`/`layersMax` from the enemy and `enemyShieldMax(run.column)`; `weaponCharge = enemyWeaponCharge(state)`; `fleeSeconds = fleeSecondsLeft(state)`; the three flags from `sysHp[...] === 0`; `hitFlash` is true for `HIT_FLASH_TICKS = 12` ticks after our last damaging or stripping hit on the enemy: the sim records `enemy.lastHitTick` (a new field, −1 while absent; set in `damageEnemy` and on a shield strip; **flattened** after `surrenderIntact`, `ENEMY_FIELDS = 22`; hashed; `SIM_VERSION = 17`, version-16 snapshots rejected) and the model computes `tick − lastHitTick < HIT_FLASH_TICKS`.

The block is built in `buildHudModel` directly from the state (not from the target rows): `enemy: state && state.enemy.present ? { hull: e.hull / e.maxHull, layers: e.shieldLayers, layersMax: enemyShieldMax(state.run.column), weaponCharge: enemyWeaponCharge(state), fleeSeconds: fleeSecondsLeft(state), weaponsDown: e.sysHp[Weapons] === 0, enginesDown: e.sysHp[Engines] === 0, shieldsDown: e.sysHp[Shields] === 0, hitFlash: e.lastHitTick >= 0 && state.tick − e.lastHitTick < HIT_FLASH_TICKS } : null` and is a member of the returned `HudModel` (every existing caller is unaffected; tests that deep-equal a whole model add `enemy: null`).

Drawing (`render/enemyPanel.ts`, Pixi `Graphics`, placed by `hud.ts`): `panel(x, y, w, h)` in `hud.ts` positions by its top-left corner, so the threat panel's left edge is `INTERNAL_W − 232`; the enemy panel is 120 × 100 with its top-left at `(INTERNAL_W − 360, 4)` (an 8 px gap to the threat panel, same top), visible only while `enemy !== null`. Inside, in panel coordinates: a hull silhouette (a 7-point polygon 56 wide and 24 tall, centred at (60, 40)) filled `DIM`, `WARN` while `hitFlash`; a shield ring of `layersMax` concentric ellipse strokes around the silhouette, centred at (60, 40) with horizontal radii 30 and 36 and vertical radii 24 and 30 (2 px strokes; the outer ring spans y 10 to 70), each drawn `TEXT` when that layer is up and `FAINT` when down, none while `shieldsDown`; a weapon bar 100 × 6 at (10, 80) with fill `weaponCharge`, `WARN` at `>= 0.8`, hidden while `weaponsDown`; a hull bar 100 × 4 at (10, 90) with fill `hull`; a 16 px label at (6, 2): `ENEMY`, or `FLEE 7s` while fleeing, or `ENGINES DOWN` while `enginesDown` (fleeing wins). The pure formatter `enemyLabel(e): string` lives in `hudModel.ts` and is tested; the drawing is covered by the manual gate, as every `hud.ts` placement is.

## 5. Sound

**Cue detector** (`app/audioCues.ts`, pure). `CueCounters` and where each field comes from (all exist today): `spawns` = the sum of `state.telemetry.spawns` (an `Int32Array(4)`, one counter per hazard type); `boardings = telemetry.boardings`; `encounters = telemetry.encounters`; `impacts = telemetry.impacts`; `shots = telemetry.shots`; `hits = telemetry.hits`; `shieldHits = telemetry.shieldHits`; `jumps = telemetry.jumps`; `ended = state.ended` (0/1); `endReason = state.endReason` (`EndReason`); `jumpReady = state.run.jumpCharge >= 1 ? 1 : 0`. `cueCounters(state): CueCounters` reads them; `audioCues(prev: CueCounters, next: CueCounters): Cue[]` returns, in this order and at most one of each per call: `'alarm'` when `spawns`, `boardings` or `encounters` rose; `'impact'` when `impacts` rose; `'shot'` when `shots` rose; `'hit'` when `hits` or `shieldHits` rose; `'ready'` when `jumpReady` went 0 → 1; `'jump'` when `jumps` rose; `'victory'` when `ended` went 0 → 1 with `endReason === Victory`; `'defeat'` when `ended` went 0 → 1 otherwise. `Cue = 'alarm' | 'impact' | 'shot' | 'hit' | 'ready' | 'jump' | 'victory' | 'defeat'`.

**Session:** `SessionDeps` gains `playCue?: (cue: Cue) => void` (optional; the tests' harness injects a collecting function, so no Electron or `AudioContext` is involved under node). The session keeps `prevCounters`, reset to `cueCounters(state)` in `start()`. In the running branch, **after the `for` loop over the frame's ticks** (the loop `break`s on the ending tick, and the detection runs once after it regardless), still inside the `try`: `const next = cueCounters(state); for (const c of audioCues(prevCounters, next)) deps.playCue?.(c); prevCounters = next;`. So the frame that ends the run emits its cues including exactly one terminal cue (`victory` or `defeat`); later frames are in the game-over phase, whose branch has no detection, so they emit nothing; the lobby and paused branches have none either. Several ticks in one frame collapse to one cue of each kind (by design).

**Synth** (`app/audio.ts`): `createAudio(): { play(cue: Cue): void; unlock(): void }` over `AudioContext` with a master `GainNode` at `MASTER_GAIN = 0.25`. Each cue is an oscillator (type, frequency, duration, gain envelope) started at `currentTime`: `alarm` square 440 → 660 Hz two 90 ms pips; `impact` sawtooth 110 Hz 180 ms with a fast decay; `shot` square 880 Hz 60 ms; `hit` triangle 1320 Hz 50 ms; `ready` sine 523 then 784 Hz, 120 ms each; `jump` sine sweep 220 → 880 Hz over 400 ms; `victory` three sines 523/659/784 Hz 150 ms each; `defeat` sawtooth 220 → 110 Hz over 600 ms. `unlock()` calls `context.resume()` inside a try/catch (a rejected promise is caught too; nothing escapes the input or focus handlers); `main.ts` wires `playCue: audio.play` and calls `audio.unlock()` on the first `keydown`/`pointerdown` and on `window` focus. `src/main/index.ts` adds `webPreferences.autoplayPolicy: 'no-user-gesture-required'` so the first alarm is not swallowed. Every `AudioContext` call sits in a try/catch; a missing `AudioContext` (tests, headless) makes `createAudio` return no-op functions. The synth is not unit-tested (no `AudioContext` under node); the detector and the session wiring are.

## 7. Tests (vitest, node)

- Repair: `failStation` then `repairStation` on Weapons with a free bar → `level 1`, `failed 0`; with the pool at 0 (every bar allocated) → `level 0`; Engineering keeps its level as today; `powerUsed` never exceeds `powerCapacity` after a repair (seeds 1-3, scripted failures); the existing power tests that pinned "comes back at level 0" are updated to the pool rule.
- HUD: `unpowered` true for a repaired console at level 0 and false at level 1, false for a failed one, always false for Engineering; the Engineering console row's `status` is `OFF` for it in `hud.ts` (model-level: the flag; the `OFF` string is the existing power-row rule). Card line 4 exact and `<= 90` characters.
- Enemy block: `null` without an enemy; with `placeEnemy` at column 5 (`layers 2`) after one strip: `{ hull: 1, layers: 1, layersMax: 2, weaponCharge: 0, fleeSeconds: 0, weaponsDown: false, ..., hitFlash: true }` on the strip tick and `hitFlash false` 12 ticks later; `enemyLabel`: `ENEMY`, `FLEE 7s`, `ENGINES DOWN`, fleeing beats engines; `lastHitTick` set by a tuned hit, a strip and a torpedo hit, −1 after `leave`, round-trips and changes the hash; version-16 rejection.
- Cues: with `Z` = all-zero counters and `at(over)` = `{ ...Z, ...over }`: `audioCues(Z, at({ spawns: 1 }))`, `audioCues(Z, at({ boardings: 1 }))`, `audioCues(Z, at({ encounters: 1 }))` each `['alarm']`; `at({ impacts: 1 })` → `['impact']`; `at({ shots: 1 })` → `['shot']`; `at({ hits: 1 })` and `at({ shieldHits: 1 })` → `['hit']`; `at({ jumpReady: 1 })` → `['ready']` and `audioCues(at({ jumpReady: 1 }), at({ jumpReady: 1 }))` → `[]`; `at({ jumps: 1 })` → `['jump']`; `at({ ended: 1, endReason: EndReason.Victory })` → `['victory']`; `at({ ended: 1, endReason: EndReason.Hull })` → `['defeat']`; `audioCues(at({ ended: 1, endReason: Hull }), at({ ended: 1, endReason: Hull }))` → `[]`; `audioCues(Z, Z)` → `[]`; the full set at once `audioCues(Z, at({ spawns: 2, boardings: 1, impacts: 1, shots: 3, hits: 1, jumpReady: 1, jumps: 1, ended: 1, endReason: Victory }))` → exactly `['alarm', 'impact', 'shot', 'hit', 'ready', 'jump', 'victory']`. `cueCounters(state)` on a state with `telemetry.spawns = [2, 1, 0, 3]` and `jumpCharge 1` gives `spawns 6`, `jumpReady 1`.
- Session (harness with `playCue` collecting into an array it returns): no cues over ten lobby frames; after start, a frame with `chargeJump` set beforehand → the frame's cues contain `'ready'` exactly once and the next frame none; a frame in which the navigator jumps (seat, `chargeJump`, Confirm) → `'jump'`; a `downCrew` ending → the ending frame's cues end with `'defeat'` exactly once and ten more game-over frames add nothing; a victory ending (column 6, `chargeJump`, seat, Confirm) → `'victory'` exactly once; `session.pause()` then ten frames → nothing; a frame whose ticks raise two alarm sources at once (`armSpawn(state)` for a hazard spawn on the next tick and `state.boarders.nextArrivalTick = state.tick + 1` for a boarding) → `'alarm'` exactly once in that frame's cues.
- Gates: survival, duo, boarding, jump, run re-run (repairs now return powered: figures move; recorded); the acceptance soak re-run and the four-player floor retried at 3; golden re-blessed once (version 17 and the repair rule; cause recorded).

## 10. Amendment: bot self-preservation

Found while running the duo gate: with consoles repaired powered, gate-duo seed 1 (237-257 s) showed both bots walking into a burning room at 241-248 s and into a second fire at 251 s, with one debris hit between; fire did most of the damage and min health stayed at 0.1. No sim tuning changes; the fix is to the bot (`bot.ts`, tests and acceptance harness only; the golden replay is unaffected).

Rule, evaluated for every bot after the urgent interactions (Fight, Revive in reach) and before the run behaviour and hazard goals, with `BOT_RETREAT_HEALTH = 30` and `BOT_RECOVER_HEALTH = 60` (bot policy constants, not tuning). A Conscious, unseated bot with `Health.cur < BOT_RETREAT_HEALTH`, or standing in the Medbay room (online, not burning) with `Health.cur < BOT_RECOVER_HEALTH`:

1. never paths into a burning room (`rooms.fire > 0`): the pathing BFS treats those tiles as impassable; if it already stands in a burning room its goal is the nearest tile of a non-burning room;
2. if the Medbay room is not burning and the Medbay station is online, its goal is the Medbay room, where it stays (neutral intent) until `Health.cur >= BOT_RECOVER_HEALTH`; the rule is stateless, position being the memory: a bot already in the online, unburning Medbay below 60 keeps staying, while a bot above 30 elsewhere does not seek the Medbay;
3. otherwise it does ordinary hazard duty subject to rule 1.

`botIntent` stays pure per tick (no memory). Result: with the stateless stay-until-60 rule, gate-duo seed 1 min health rises from 0.1 to 28.8 (it was 10.0 with an earlier one-shot retreat), so every seed keeps the 25 floor (seed 1 was the motivating case: two debris-started fires and one impact in 237-257 s).


## 11. Amendments from the plan and the implementation
- The version bump to 17 is for `enemy.lastHitTick`; the repair rule alone changes no state shape. The enemy flatten order ends `... fleeing, surrenderIntact, lastHitTick` (`ENEMY_FIELDS = 22`).
- The repair rule broke `gate-duo` seed 1; the bot self-preservation rule of section 10 (not a sim lever) is the fix, after the debris-damage lever was tried and reverted with evidence that fire did most of the damage.
- Card line 4 is `Hold Interact at a fire, breach, failed console or downed friend. Repairs need a bar` (84 characters; the spec's first text was 105 and broke the 90-character budget).
- `HIT_FLASH_TICKS = 12` lives in `tuning.ts` and is read only by the HUD model (not hashed).
- `createEnemyPanel(fontFamily = 'm5x7')` is called once by `createHud`, added at `(INTERNAL_W − 360, 4)` and updated every frame; its background is a plain rect, not the nine-slice panel; the weapon track stays visible (faint) while the enemy's weapons are down and only the fill hides. The drawing is a manual gate.
- The session's `prevCounters` is nullable and guarded until the first `start()`; the two-alarm-sources session test uses one 100 ms frame so both sources land in a single frame.
- The pool-emptying test loops `shiftBar` over every non-Engineering station until none accepts a bar.
