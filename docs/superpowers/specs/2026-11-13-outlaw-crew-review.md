# Outlaw Crew direction review — 2026-11-13

The direction is coherent and worth carrying forward. It is not yet a reliable milestone contract: several claimed carry-overs conceal new simulation work, and the integration milestones need smaller playable gates. This review accepts the owner's task-loop, combat, dialogue, open-world and 1–16-player direction.

Reviewed the direction spec, roadmap, Net-0, Net-1 including amendments, and relevant simulation/rendering code. Code paths below are relative to `src/renderer/src/`. This was static inspection; no tests, installs or gameplay sessions were run.

1. **High — The mini-game commands do not fit Net-1 unchanged (§8).** Net-1 §5 defines `fire` with station, weapon and target, without quality; there is no `repair` command. Repair remains positional held input (§6). Today's repair accumulates progress and XP per tick (`sim/interact.ts:152`); fire consumes charge/ammo and rolls accuracy (`sim/weapons.ts:105`). A quality-bearing result changes those contracts and gameplay, contrary to Net-1's parity requirement. **Fix:** keep Net-1a unchanged as an extraction, but explicitly schedule an MG command/protocol extension afterward: typed task/attempt and target identities, bounded quality, execution phase, completion effects, cancellation and duplicate handling. Replace or deliberately retain held repair; never allow both paths to pay out independently.

2. **High — Client results need an attempt lifecycle, not just bounds (§§6, 8).** Seat/timing/bounds checks alone cannot establish that a result belongs to the still-active task, weapon, damage instance or difficulty. Standing, switching tabs, death, repaired targets, pauses, reconnects and host recovery can invalidate a 12-second attempt. Unique command IDs prevent packet duplication, but not repeated rewards under new IDs. **Fix:** authority-owned attempt identity and eligibility/start tick, minimum duration, one completion, and explicit invalidation rules. Pin difficulty/skill at an agreed point. Record canonical quality and accepted execution tick in replay; put active attempts and bot timing/RNG state in checkpoints. Use a defined numeric representation and deterministic outcome formula; client animation need not replay. Bounds cannot prove honest dexterity: explicitly accept that friends-only trust limit. Test duplicate, stale, interrupted and restored completions and delayed delivery.

3. **High — “Both fully simulated” overstates existing combat (§§3, 6a, 8).** Enemy systems are four HP/ion entries and five rooms (`sim/enemy.ts:24`); repair is a timer (`sim/enemy.ts:181`), and visible crew are explicitly render-only (`render/enemyWindowModel.ts:74`). Enemy fires/breaches are damage timers (`sim/enemy.ts:145`), not the player's oxygen/door/reactor/crew simulation. Boarding combat currently operates in the player's rooms (`sim/boarders.ts:26`). **Fix:** distinguish the retained two-ship combat mechanics from new symmetric enemy simulation, outbound boarding and capture. Give these explicit milestones and acceptance criteria; assign full enemy simulation before the milestone claiming it. Do not present it as a UI replacement or postpone its cost invisibly into OW-2.

4. **High — Task scheduling and station rules are unspecified (§§3a, 6, 9).** The Skiff deliberately groups Navigation/Weapons and Science/Engineering/Doors at two seats (`sim/ship/skiff.json:46`), contradicting the universal one-room/one-station description. Three-to-six tasks, repeated station actions and a shared bar lack authoritative definitions, ownership and completion conditions. Two-room crises can deadlock a lone conscious survivor. **Fix:** define a minimal task model: stable instance, leg/goal, room, assignee/claim, prerequisites, progress, reward and lifecycle. Specify reassignment, partial completion, combat suspension/resumption, and bar scaling with active crew. State the Skiff's grouping exception and where roaming tasks live. Define solo/bot and incapacitation recovery for paired crises. Decide whether upkeep decay stops at port or during dialogue.

5. **High — MG-0 tests controls, not the central combat risk (§§9–10).** A standalone page is the right cheap first usability test, but cannot test eyes-off-ship pressure, interruptions or cooperation. MG-1 then bundles task scheduling, every console, crises, bots and combat. Skills currently multiply station output and repair speed (`sim/stations.ts:13`, `sim/skills.ts:22`); making them ease puzzles *instead* is a balance change. Adding a 3–12-second action can also unintentionally extend every weapon cycle. **Fix:** retain MG-0 as a disposable prototype that may parallel Net-1a; follow with an integrated Weapons/Sensors/Repair encounter before full console conversion. Define quality effects, charge overlap, passive output while unoccupied, XP, teamwork and retained/replaced skill multipliers. Gate on combat throughput, crisis response and repeated-play fatigue; split MG-1 into playable increments.

6. **High — The milestone dependency chain loses required work (§9).** Net-1b is absent, although Net-0 requires it before internet play and it owns durable checkpoints and per-viewer HUD. Replacing Net-2 also leaves click-to-move/pathfinding without an owner. Removing sectors before OW-1 leaves MG-1/EV-1 without a session structure. OW-1 combines markets, contracts, travel, two backgrounds, two new stations and saving. **Fix:** explicitly retain Net-1b before Net-3 and require its durability foundation for campaign saving; assign Net-2's controls/layout deliverables to MG milestones. Keep the sector run as a temporary playable harness through MG-1/EV-1, then replace it with a bounded port-to-port contract slice. Expand OW-1 afterward. Label MG-0 a prototype, not a complete playable game milestone.

7. **Medium — Bot quality is only one part of solo viability (§§3a, 6, 8).** Existing bots prioritize rescue, recovery, gunner/science duties and navigation (`sim/bot.ts:306`); they have neither task assignments nor paired-panel coordination. An instantaneous quality roll could outperform humans and change combat cadence. **Fix:** add travel, reservations, attempt duration, interruption, crisis pairing and deterministic quality to the bot contract. Use the same validation as humans, with no free completions. Gate solo-with-bots, a lone survivor, mixed human/bot crews and disconnect/reclaim mid-task. Preserve the Net-1 bot baseline until MG deliberately changes it.

8. **Medium — Dialogue needs authoritative state and a vote policy (§§3a, 6b, 9).** The event interpreter and safe choices carry over, but `EventDef` is a single choice set (`sim/eventDefs.ts:35`). Requirements cover resources, stations and conscious crew skills, not abilities, relationships or named qualifying crew (`sim/events.ts:42`). Today's tally selects the highest count, breaking ties with Navigation, not a defined captain; its timeout starts at the first vote (`sim/run.ts:326`, `sim/run.ts:349`). **Fix:** add beat identity to vote references, reset votes per beat, revalidate qualifiers at resolution, and specify conscious/away crew eligibility. Assign captain selection/fallback, timer and combat-pause policy. Prototype a branching conversation before rewriting all 28 events; assign contacts and relationship persistence to a milestone.

9. **Medium — Couch and campaign scale need earlier gates (§§7–10).** Four simultaneous panels, ship visibility, enemy targeting and dialogue compete for screen space and input focus. Existing snapshots preserve one simulation (`sim/snapshot.ts:14`), not campaign ownership, changing rosters, debt and persistent places. Director pacing is beacon-gated and elapsed-time based (`sim/director.ts:26`, `sim/director.ts:209`). **Fix:** test two/four-player controller and keyboard layouts at the target resolution during MG prototyping, including alerts and accessible timing. Define campaign save ownership, reserved-seat/roster changes, loss recovery, economy bounds and voyage pacing before OW-1. Separate SHIP-2's capital ship from fleet play; validate task occupancy, votes, bots and measured network/state costs at 6 and 16 players instead of assuming a cap increase suffices.

10. **Low — Originality is a policy, not yet demonstrated (§§2, 4, 6, 10).** No copied finished asset is supplied here. Generic valve, waveform and circuit puzzles do not establish a distinct presentation, and “all designs ... original” is unsupported at this stage. **Fix:** require original interaction sketches, room topology, terminology, silhouettes and portraits at each content gate; compare actual prototypes with the cited inspirations. Treat the named references as design context and the setting names as provisional. This is a content review, not name clearance.

Section 11 recommendations: **(1)** Agree with “Insurgent” and the stated mission tone; remove the unsupported assurance about storefront/rating outcomes. **(2)** Agree with persistent campaigns plus optional one-life mode, subject to explicit save, roster and loss rules. **(3)** Agree with retaining 2–4-player couch play, with the early readability/input gate above. **(4)** Agree with placeholders until owner naming; review final names and assets separately. **(5)** Agree with parking the traitor mode: it would need a separate secrecy, voting and trust design.

Summary: preserve the direction and Net-1a extraction. Make the MG command extension explicit, correct the enemy-simulation baseline, restore missing dependencies, and prove a small integrated task/combat loop before expanding content and campaign scope.
