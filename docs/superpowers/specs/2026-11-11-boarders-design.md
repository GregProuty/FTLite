# Boarders (Boarders-i) — Design

Status: approved 2026-11-11 under the autonomous feature loop (`docs/roadmap.md` item 7); every open choice took the recommended option; amended the same day from the Codex spec reviews (`research/codex-review-boarders-spec-1.md`, `-2.md`). Parent: `2026-11-09-foundation-design.md` §1 (sub-project 3, boarders and crew-vs-crew combat), §5 Crew (the Combat skill "has no source of XP or effect in the Foundation, because there are no enemies until boarders"), §5 Crew lifecycle, §5 Stations (Doors, Medbay). Builds on 3-i (skills and XP), 4-i (director stress and attendance), 4-ii (telemetry), Combat-i/ii (the enemy; its tuning fallbacks). This is the first and, for the ten-feature run, the only boarders feature.

## 1. Goal

Put enemies **inside the ship**. A **boarding party** of two boarders arrives in a room on a schedule. Boarders are room-level agents: each occupies a room, fights any Conscious crew member there, hunts through unsealed doors toward crew when a room is empty, and sabotages a console when left alone. Crew fight back by **holding Interact** in the boarder's room (a new, highest-priority Interact target), with damage dealt and damage taken scaled by the **Combat** skill, which finally earns XP. **Doors** become a containment tool: a sealed door stops a boarder; the Doors console and local seals already exist. The **Medbay** is the recovery loop: it heals the survivors, unchanged. A dead boarder vanishes; a downed crew member is revived as today. No RNG is drawn in a fight (damage is deterministic per tick), so a melee is reproducible from the replay without new streams.

**Out of scope:** boarders as tile-walking sprites (they are drawn as a room marker), boarders breaking sealed doors, boarders affected by fire or oxygen, boarder weapons or ranged fire, a crew sidearm, boarders tied to the enemy ship's presence, loot, more than one party aboard at a time.

## 2. Decisions taken (recommended options)

| Question | Options considered | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Where a boarder is | (a) a tile-walking entity with pathing; (b) a **room index** with a move timer; (c) attached to a crew member | **(b)**: the room graph (`derived.edgeA/edgeB`, `edgeIsDoor`, `doors.state`) already exists, movement is one deterministic choice every `BOARDER_MOVE` seconds, and sealing a door blocks it for free. |
| Combat resolution | (a) hit rolls from `rng.combat`; (b) **deterministic damage per tick** | **(b)**: no new draws, melee replays exactly, skill shows as a rate. |
| Who a boarder attacks | the Conscious crew member in its room with the **lowest pid** (deterministic focus), `BOARDER_DPS` per second reduced by the target's Combat defence | A focused boarder is dangerous and legible; two crew in the room split nothing but both can hit it. |
| How crew fight | a new Interact target `Fight` (the boarder in the player's room with the lowest `StableId`), **above Revive** in priority; holding Interact deals `CREW_MELEE_DPS × (1 + COMBAT_GAIN × level)` per second; seated crew do not fight | A boarder in the room is the most urgent thing in it. |
| Combat XP | `XP_RATE` per second of fighting (the Repair pattern) plus `XP_PER_KILL` to the crew member whose damage killed the boarder | Combat gets its source of XP. |
| Hunting | every `BOARDER_MOVE` (8 s) a boarder whose room has no Conscious crew moves through an **unsealed door** (closed or open) to the adjacent room with Conscious crew (lowest room index among them), else to a uniform random adjacent unsealed room (`rng.hazards`), else stays; a boarder in a room with Conscious crew stays and fights (its move clock re-arms) | Sealed doors contain; the crew can fort up. |
| Sabotage | a boarder alone in a room (no Conscious crew) **with an online console present** for `BOARDER_SABOTAGE` (15 s) continuous fails the lowest-index online console there once per visit (`failStation`, the existing power-failure hazard, repaired as today); the clock resets whenever crew enter or no online console remains, and a visit that has sabotaged cannot sabotage again until the boarder moves away and returns | A contained or ignored boarder still costs something, once. |
| Arrival | `BOARDING_FIRST` (240 s) then `BOARDING_COOLDOWN` (180 s) × jitter after the last boarder dies, only while **no** boarder is aboard; the party of `BOARDING_PARTY` (2) lands in one uniform random room (`rng.hazards`); the jitter is one `rng.director` draw | One party at a time; thematically a boarding pod, independent of the enemy ship so it can be tested alone. |
| Director | `STRESS_WEIGHTS.boarder` (0.35) per boarder aboard; a boarder with no Conscious crew in its room is **unattended**; boarders do not count toward the spawn cap (`activeHazardCount` unchanged) | Boarders are pressure, not a spawn. |
| HUD | threat row `Boarders: <room> x2` (`icon.sensors`); player prompt `Fight boarder` with the boarder's health fraction as the progress bar (it drains); a hostile **marker** drawn at the boarder's room centre in the world view (a Graphics diamond, no new asset) | No new panel, no new art. |
| Bot | the bot already holds Interact when it has a target, so it fights a boarder in its room; it also walks toward rooms with boarders after downed teammates and before fires | The survival gate's crew must respond or it cannot pass. |
| Survival gate levers (pre-authorised, per the Combat-ii review) | before the single golden re-bless, in order: (1) `DEBRIS_CREW_DAMAGE` 20 → 15 (shared by debris and enemy shots today); (2) a separate `ENEMY_SHOT_CREW_DAMAGE` 12 (shots stop sharing the debris value); (3) `BOARDER_DPS` 6 → 4; (4) `BOARDER_HEALTH` 60 → 45; (5) `BOARDING_FIRST` 240 → 330 (past the five-minute window), **only** as the last resort, and the dedicated boarding gate (§9) must still pass | The baseline run already shows seed 2 at about 65/53 health at 4:00 and a 0.1 minimum at 4:46; a two-fighter clearance costs the focused bot about 45 to 68 health, so softening the fight comes before hiding it. A separate boarding gate with a forced early boarding exists so the feature is always exercised in a gate, whatever `BOARDING_FIRST` ends up as. |
| Melee arithmetic | health is `max(0, health − amount)` and then snapped to 0 when below `1e-9`, so 450 blows of 8/60 kill a 60-health boarder on the 450th blow rather than leaving `1.7e-13` behind | Exact tick counts in the tests and the record. |

## 4. Authoritative state

```ts
boarders: {
  active: Uint8Array;        // BOARDER_SLOTS
  stableId: Int32Array;      // 0 while inactive
  room: Int16Array;          // room index
  health: Float64Array;      // 0..BOARDER_HEALTH
  nextMoveTick: Int32Array;  // when the boarder may move if its room is empty of Conscious crew
  aloneSince: Int32Array;    // tick the sabotage clock started in the current room; -1 while the clock is not running; -2 once this visit has sabotaged (until the boarder moves)
  nextArrivalTick: number;   // BOARDING_FIRST x TICK_HZ at creation; re-armed when the last boarder dies
};
telemetry.boardings: number; telemetry.boardersKilled: number;
```

Flattened in a `boarders` manifest section after `enemy`: `nextArrivalTick`, then the six arrays in the order above (`BOARDERS_FIELDS = 1 + 6 × BOARDER_SLOTS`); the two telemetry scalars after `enemyDisables` (`TELEMETRY_SCALARS = 15`). `SIM_VERSION` becomes 12; version-11 snapshots are rejected. Initial values: all 0, `aloneSince` all −1, `nextArrivalTick = BOARDING_FIRST × TICK_HZ`. The `aloneSince` sentinel `-2` is part of the authoritative state and survives a snapshot (the continuation test covers a sabotaged visit).

## 5. Rules

**Per tick** (`boarderSystem`, after `debrisSystem`, before `medbayStep`):

1. **Arrival.** If `activeBoarderCount === 0` and `tick >= nextArrivalTick`: draw the room `nextInt(rng.hazards, roomCount)` (one draw); for each of `BOARDING_PARTY` free slots in ascending slot order: `active 1`, `stableId = nextStableId++`, `room`, `health = BOARDER_HEALTH`, `nextMoveTick = tick + BOARDER_MOVE × TICK_HZ`, `aloneSince = -1`; `telemetry.boardings += 1`. `nextArrivalTick` is left as is (it is re-armed on the last death). Processing then **continues into step 2 on the same tick**: a party landing in a room with crew attacks on the arrival tick (the crew's own blows start the next tick, since `interactSystem` has already run), and a party landing in an empty room starts its solitude clock on the arrival tick; no boarder can move on the arrival tick because `nextMoveTick` is in the future.
2. **For each active boarder in ascending slot order:**
   - Let `targets` be the Conscious crew members whose `roomAt` equals the boarder's room, and `console` the **lowest station index** with `stationRoom === room && stationOnline` (−1 if none).
   - If `targets` is non-empty: **fight**: the lowest-pid target loses `BOARDER_DPS × DT × (1 − combatDefence(level))` health (clamped at 0; at 0 `downCrew` as debris does); `nextMoveTick = tick + BOARDER_MOVE × TICK_HZ`; if `aloneSince >= 0` set `aloneSince = -1` (a `-2` is kept: the visit has already sabotaged).
   - Else (alone): the sabotage clock measures **continuous solitude with an eligible console**: if `console < 0`, set `aloneSince = -1` unless it is `-2`; else if `aloneSince === -1` set `aloneSince = tick`; else if `aloneSince >= 0 && tick − aloneSince >= BOARDER_SABOTAGE × TICK_HZ`: `failStation(console)` and `aloneSince = -2` (one sabotage per visit; eligible again only after the boarder moves away and returns). Then if `tick >= nextMoveTick`: **move** (below) and `nextMoveTick = tick + BOARDER_MOVE × TICK_HZ`.
3. **Move:** candidates are the rooms adjacent through a door edge (`edgeIsDoor`) whose `doors.state !== Sealed`, in ascending edge index with duplicates removed, excluding the current room. If any candidate contains a Conscious crew member, move to the lowest such room index (no draw). Else if candidates are non-empty, move to `candidates[nextInt(rng.hazards, candidates.length)]` (one draw). Else stay (no draw, `aloneSince` untouched). An actual room change sets `aloneSince = -1`.

**Crew fighting** (`interactSystem`, step 1 of the tick): `interactTarget` returns `{ kind: Fight, index: slot }` for the active boarder in the player's room with the lowest `StableId`, **before** the Revive check; everything else is unchanged. A holder with a Fight target deals `combatDps(skillLevelOf(pid, Combat)) × DT` to that boarder through `damageBoarder(state, slot, amount, pid)`, gains `XP_RATE × DT` Combat XP and sets `scratch.activeThisTick[pid] = 1` (fighting is activity for telemetry). Several holders add their damage in pid order; once the boarder is dead later holders in the same tick see `active 0` and `continue` (as repairs do). A seated or non-Conscious player has no target (as today).

`damageBoarder(state, slot, amount, pid)`: `health = max(0, health − amount)`, then `if (health < HEALTH_EPS) health = 0` (`HEALTH_EPS = 1e-9`, so accumulated float error never leaves a boarder alive at `1.7e-13`); at 0: `active 0`, `stableId 0`, `telemetry.boardersKilled += 1`, `addXp(pid, Combat, XP_PER_KILL)` (the killer is the holder whose blow reached 0, so exactly one award per boarder; `addXp` applies the character's XP multiplier, so a Slow Learner gets 0.75 of both the per-second and the kill XP); if no boarder remains, one `rng.director` draw arms `nextArrivalTick = tick + round(BOARDING_COOLDOWN × TICK_HZ × (1 + BOARDING_JITTER × (2u − 1)))`. The same snap applies to a crew member's health under boarder damage (`downCrew` at exactly 0).

**Same-tick ordering of a melee:** the crew's blows land in `interactSystem` (step 1) and the boarders' blows in `boarderSystem` (step 5c), so on the tick a boarder dies it deals no damage; a boarder deals damage to exactly one target per tick, and when that target is downed by the blow the focus moves to the next lowest Conscious pid **on the next tick**. Fighting draws nothing from any RNG stream.

`combatDps(level) = CREW_MELEE_DPS × (1 + COMBAT_GAIN × level)`; `combatDefence(level) = COMBAT_DEFENCE × level` (0.4 at level 10).

**Director:** `rawStress` gains `w.boarder × activeBoarderCount`; `anyUnattended` returns true for an active boarder whose room has no Conscious crew member. `activeHazardCount` is unchanged.

**Interactions kept simple:** boarders ignore fire, oxygen, breaches and debris; they are not healed; they do not open or close doors (a closed door is passable to them as it is to crew; a sealed one is not); they never attack Downed or Dead crew (they move on); a boarder in the Medbay room fights crew there like anywhere else. The run-end rules are unchanged (a boarder downing the last Conscious crew member ends the run through `crewSystem`).

## 6. Bot

`goalTiles`: after the downed-teammate early return and **before** any hazard goal is collected, if any boarder is aboard return the set of every tile of every room holding an active boarder (its own early return, so boarders outrank fires, breaches and failures the way downed teammates do; the existing hazard goals are a combined set chosen by BFS distance, so insertion order alone would not give priority). The bot already holds Interact whenever `interactTarget` is not `None`, so in the room it fights. (It walks into a boarder's room on purpose; that is the intended "crew defends the ship" behaviour and what the survival gate measures.)

## 7. HUD and render

- Threat rows (after the enemy row, before fires): one per room with boarders, `Boarders: <room> x<n>` with `icon.sensors`, rooms in ascending index.
- Player panel: with a Fight target, `interactPrompt = 'Fight boarder'`, `interactIcon = null`, `progress = boarder health / BOARDER_HEALTH` (the bar drains as the boarder weakens). The Combat skill line already shows `C<level>`.
- `boarderMarkers(state): { stableId: number; x: number; y: number; health: number }[]` is a **pure projection in `sim/boarders.ts`**, built from `roomCentre(state, room)` (the mean of the room's tile centres: `Σ(tx + 0.5) / n`, `Σ(ty + 0.5) / n`) plus a slot offset `(slot − 1.5) × 0.4` on x so two boarders in one room do not overlap; `health` is the fraction. On the starter ship room A's centroid is `(3, 2.5)`, so slots 0 and 1 in A sit at `(2.4, 2.5)` and `(2.8, 2.5)`; room G's centroid is `(4, 9.5)`. `HudModel.boarders` is that array (empty with no state).
- Render: the renderer's `draw(state, alpha)` runs before the session builds the HUD model and the crew layer receives only the state, so the markers are drawn by a new world layer `render/boarderLayer.ts` (`createBoarderLayer(): { container, draw(state) }`) added to the world container above the crew layer and called from `renderer.draw` with the state; it calls the same pure `boarderMarkers(state)` and draws one red diamond (Graphics, 10 px) per entry, keyed by `stableId` through `syncCache` like the crew circles; no asset, no fallback path needed (Graphics only), no animation (manual gate).

## 8. Tuning (first pass, all hypotheses)

```ts
SIM_VERSION = 12
BOARDER_SLOTS = 4
BOARDING_FIRST = 240, BOARDING_COOLDOWN = 180, BOARDING_JITTER = 0.2   // seconds
BOARDING_PARTY = 2
BOARDER_HEALTH = 60
BOARDER_DPS = 6                 // to the focused crew member, before Combat defence
CREW_MELEE_DPS = 8              // at Combat level 0
COMBAT_GAIN = 0.06              // dps x (1 + gain x level): x1.6 at level 10
COMBAT_DEFENCE = 0.04           // damage taken x (1 - defence x level): x0.6 at level 10
BOARDER_MOVE = 8                // seconds between moves while the room is empty
BOARDER_SABOTAGE = 15           // seconds alone with an online console before it fails
XP_PER_KILL = 20                // Combat XP to the killer
STRESS_WEIGHTS.boarder = 0.35
```

Worked numbers (level 0, first-pass values): one crew member kills a boarder on the 450th blow (7.5 s) and, because the killing tick deals no incoming damage, loses `449 × 0.1 = 44.9` health (55.1 left from 100); two crew members kill it on the 225th blow and the focused one loses 22.4 (77.6 left); a party of two costs a lone fighter about 90 health, so a solo fight is a near-death and a pair fight is the design; the solo killer earns `450 × XP_RATE / 60 + XP_PER_KILL = 27.5` Combat XP (level 1 needs 240: about nine solo kills). A crew member who stands in a room with two boarders and does nothing loses 12 health per second and is Downed in about 8 s. The Medbay (4 health per second at full output) restores a 45-health loss in about 11 s. Sealing a door costs nothing and buys as long as the seal holds. In the five-minute survival gate the first party lands at 4:00 with the crew already worn by the enemy (seed 2 is at about 65/53 health then), and a two-bot clearance costs the focused bot 45 to 68 health depending on how the two bots arrive; the pre-authorised levers (§2) exist for that.

**later holders skip a dead boarder:** two fighters, boarder at `8/60 + 1e-6` health (one blow from pid 0 kills it), one tick: pid 0 earns the kill XP and the per-tick XP, pid 1 earns no Combat XP that tick and `activeThisTick[1] === 0`, `boardersKilled 1`; **RNG neutrality:** the director, hazards and combat stream states are unchanged across a 100-tick fight (a fixture with `quiet` and both schedules silenced); `interactTarget` with a boarder and a Downed teammate in the room returns Fight, and Revive once the boarder is dead; a seated crew member in the boarder's room is attacked (health falls) but has no Fight target and is **not** stood up; the focused target is the lowest Conscious pid; **immediate downing and focus transfer:** pid 0 at health 0.05 and pid 1 at 100 in the boarder's room, one tick: pid 0 is Downed (`downCrew`: stood, `bleed` set) and pid 1 is still at 100; the next tick pid 1 is at 99.9; a Downed crew member alone with a boarder is ignored (the boarder's clock runs and it moves at `nextMoveTick`); **last Conscious:** a solo Recruit at 0.05 health downed by a boarder ends the run through `crewSystem` on that tick (`ended 1`, `endReason Crew`); **Medbay ordering:** a Recruit standing in the Medbay room (F, online at the starter allocation) with a boarder there and `interactHeld`: after one tick health is `100 − 0.1 + MEDBAY_HEAL × stationOutput(Medbay) × DT` (the heal lands the same tick as the blow); a boarder standing in a burning room or in the Medbay room keeps exactly its health (immune to fire, never healed); `scratch.activeThisTick` is set for a fighter (telemetry `idleRun 0`).

- **Bot:** with a boarder in G and the bot in A (no other goals), `botIntent` moves toward A's door (the first BFS step toward I); in the boarder's room it returns `interactHeld`; **priorities:** a Downed teammate adjacent and a boarder elsewhere → the bot revives (`interactHeld`, no move); a boarder in G and a fire in the bot's own room A → the bot repairs the fire (the local Interact target wins, as it does today for every movement goal: this is the existing policy and is kept); a boarder in G and a fire in B (both one hop away through I) → the bot's goals are G's tiles only (the fire is not a goal while boarders are aboard).
- **HUD:** the threat row text and icon for one room with two boarders (`Boarders: Bridge x2`) and for two rooms (ascending room index, after the enemy row and before fires); the player prompt `Fight boarder`, `interactIcon null`, `progress 0.5` for a boarder at 30; `boarderMarkers` for two boarders in A gives `[(2.4, 2.5), (2.8, 2.5)]` with `health` fractions, and `HudModel.boarders` equals it; `boarders: []` with no state and with no boarder.
