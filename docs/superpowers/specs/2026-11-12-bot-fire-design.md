# Bot Fire Handling (Demo-v) Design

Status: 2026-11-12, the user chose "ship the door fix, bot work next". Parent: Demo-ii spec section 10 (bot self-preservation).

## Problem (evidence from per-second logs of `gate-duo` seed 3 after door corner sliding, 2026-11-12)

1. **Doorway blind spot.** `retreatIntent` asks `roomAt` which room the bot is in; a door tile has none, but fire damage (`conditionRoomAt`) counts a doorway as the worse neighbouring room. A hurt bot standing in a burning doorway therefore takes fire damage while its retreat rule thinks it is safe, and stands still (seed 3: 32 to 0 health at (9.5, 7.0) beside the burning corridor).
2. **Idle while the ship burns.** With every route blocked by fire and the Medbay unusable, a hurt bot's ordinary duty search (which avoids burning rooms) finds nothing and the bot idles while fires spread until both crew die.
3. **Dithering.** Patching (1) and (2) independently made a hurt bot flip every tick at a doorway: in the doorway the escape rule sends it out; one step out, the duty fallback sends it back toward the fire.

Four ad-hoc patches were tried and reverted; this spec replaces them with one consistent policy.

## Requirements

- One policy, no flip-flop: for a fixed state a bot's choice must not alternate tick to tick between two neighbouring tiles. A new test drives a hurt bot at a burning doorway with no safe Medbay for 600 ticks and asserts it either leaves the area for good or holds Interact on the fire, and never reverses its movement direction more than twice.
- Doorways count as the room whose conditions hurt there (`conditionRoomAt`) for every "am I in fire" decision; the Medbay-presence decision keeps `roomAt` (healing applies only inside the room).
- A hurt bot never idles indefinitely while a fire burns that it could reach: if no safe Medbay route exists, it does hazard duty (fights fires), choosing deterministically.
- The existing bot tests (a)-(g) in `tests/bot.test.ts` keep their meaning; if one conflicts with the new policy, the conflict is stated and resolved in this spec's amendments with the reason (do not silently weaken a test).
- `gate-duo` seed 3 floor back to 25 (remove the `seed === 3 ? 0 : 25` exception and its comment); every other gate passes; the golden replay unchanged (the bot is not in the sim path).

## Amendments

**Status 2026-11-12: BLOCKED on the seed-3 floor.** The policy below fixes the three behaviours and passes every bot test and every other gate, but `gate-duo` seed 3 reaches only 16.8 (from 0.0), so the floor stays at the "both survive" exception and nothing is committed. See the analysis below.

**Policy (one rule, stateless, in `retreatIntent`).** A hurt bot (Conscious, unseated, below `BOT_RETREAT_HEALTH`, or in the online unburning Medbay below `BOT_RECOVER_HEALTH`) works through these steps in order. (1) If it is in the Medbay (`roomAt`), it stays. (2) If a fire-free route to the Medbay exists (every burning room blocked), it walks it. (3) If no other Conscious crew member *covers* the fires, it does hazard duty with nothing blocked, so it fights the fires. (4) Otherwise, if it is in fire (`conditionRoomAt`, so a doorway counts as the burning room beside it), it gets out to the nearest non-burning room. If it is trapped, it fights the fire it stands in, and failing that it takes the shortest way out through the fire. (5) Otherwise it does ordinary duty that avoids every burning room, which means waiting. A teammate *covers* when it is at or above 30 and not healing in the Medbay, or when it outranks this bot by (already in fire, then health, then lower pid). Position is the memory: a hurt bot that has reached a fire keeps the fighter's role, so the role does not swap back and forth as its health falls, and the bot never flips between "escape" and "go back" (the escape rule applies only to covered bots, and those never path into fire).

**Tests.** (a) pid 0 at 100 covers, so the hurt bot waits outside the corridor and repairs once the fire is out. (b) The teammate covers, so it escapes the burning room. (c) Step 2, then step 1. (d) Revive is urgent and outranks everything. (e) At 80 it is not hurt, so ordinary duty applies. (f) Covered and trapped (the corridor burns too), so it fights its own room. (g) pid 1 covers while above 30; once pid 1 falls below 30 fighting the endless test fire, pid 1 is in fire and outranks the captain, so the captain still waits. New (h): doorway blind spot; it steps out of a burning doorway within 60 ticks. New (i): the anti-dithering test at a burning doorway with the Medbay offline, 600 ticks, fire spread suppressed so the state is fixed, at most two direction reversals. With a teammate at 100 it leaves for good (steps 4 and 5). With a teammate at 20 it holds Interact in the corridor (step 3). No existing test needed changing.

**Why seed 3 stays below 25.** Door corner sliding changes the trajectory from 46 s on (a movement change, before any fire), and the new run is fire-heavy: eight fires between 161 and 207 s, compared with 30 burning seconds in the whole pre-sliding run. Per-second logs: with the doorway fix alone (healing teammates counted as cover), seed 3 reached 7.8. The corridor then burned unattended for 25 s while p1 healed, it spread, and a debris impact in the Medbay at 238 s took 13-15 hp from both bots. With the policy above (a healing teammate is not cover), the minimum is 16.8: p0 at 26 is caught by a fresh corridor ignition at 203 s, nobody covers (p1 is healing at 33), and it puts the fire out in 4 s, dropping to 16.8. Escaping instead lets the fire spread (that was the 7.8 run). Variants tried and dropped: a healing bot leaving the Medbay to fight when uncovered (crew died; it also broke (c), because the endless test fire wears the healthy teammate below 30); a hurt bot finishing the fire it stands in before healing (0.05); ranking by health divided by the Flammable multiplier (no change, 16.8). Further gains are not available from fire handling: the loss is fire exposure that some bot must take. Candidates outside this spec: dodge warned debris (bots ignore the warning today, and it cost 20 per crew member in the 7.8 run); a lever re-authorisation; or accepting a seed-3 floor with the reason.
