## 4. Deviations from the plan or spec

1. **The mapping offset.** After each confirm the mapping offset is `target − newest local tick` (plan deviation 1). With it, `tick()`'s own-entry steps and the verify mapping agree under the 2-tick slack.
2. **Log entries leave only by acknowledgement.** That means at or before the anchor, or by `PREDICT_LOG_MAX` (plan deviation 2). The spec's §3.4 step 1 trim drops entries before their held watermark returns, so it never builds a lead. This was found in plan review (R1-F1, P0).
3. **Reseat corrections are counted apart from G4's half-tile bound.** A correction that flips this seat's seated state (`correction.reseated`) is a refused action taken back, as §3.7 allows. The cause: two held frames latched in one authority tick, so the authority walked one step fewer, and the predicted sit was refused as out of reach. The character then snaps back about 1.8 tiles. The rule: at most 3 per seat in the 60 s run, at most 2 tiles each. The spec's §6 G4 row now says so.
4. **`gunManualNow`, a one-pump value in the session.** R plus the autocannon trigger in one pump fires on that tick. The sim applies `gunMode` before the gun fires in the same tick.
5. **A test that now expects the opposite.** The crew Helm test "A does not clear the held heading" now expects A to clear it. That matches the sim and the owner's 2026-11-19 A/D-turn decision.
6. **Fire crackle reads the confirmed interior.** It lags by the lead. Fires come from enemy hits, so a prediction would not show them earlier.
7. **`helmVent?` is gone from `DuelViewSource`.** No source provided it any more.

## 6. Fable fixes

The fix wave from the Fable review (fix list `.superpowers/sdd/fable-fix-list.md`). All figures are from the headless full stack (`tests/predictionGates.test.ts`, `tests/predictionCorrections.test.ts`).

### The verified share (Fable #3)

The predictor dropped acknowledged log entries even when P stood a tick or two past the measured target, inside the slack. P's next ticks map to exactly those entries, so `planned()` used the newest held input instead, and the publication re-simulated. Now an entry leaves only when it is at or before both the last anchor and T. A kept entry never anchors again: only a newer held sequence does.

At 100 ms with ±5 ms jitter and this seat's input changing, over 600 ticks:

| Case | Before | After | Spread over 7 link seeds (after) | Floor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1b2, even pump | 29.1% | 70.9% | 67–75% | 55% |
| G1b3, a pump of 0 then 2 local ticks every 5th tick | 38.5% | 75.0% | 64–75% | 55% |

The floor is 9 points under the lowest seed and 16 over the higher "before" figure. The reviewer's own run measured 33% before and 81% after. The rest of the re-simulations are real: when a frame is latched one tick early or late while the input changes, the step really did use different input.

### Heading corrections (Fable #1), in heading steps

These come from the G4 scripted pair at ±5 ms jitter, after the first second. Seat 0 flies the crew Helm and seat 1 sits at Weapons.

| Round trip | Helm seat p95 | Helm seat max | Weapons seat p95 | Weapons seat max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 ms | 0 | 0 | 42.7 | 48.0 |
| 100 ms | 7.1 | 42.7 | 64.0 | 76.4 |
| 200 ms | 7.1 | 78.2 | 71.1 | 120.9 |

- **The Helm seat.** 7.11 steps is one tick of the scripted Helm's boosted aim turn, so each of these corrections is a held frame that jitter moved by one authority tick. G4 bounds the Helm seat's p95 at 8 steps.
- **Why not the fix list's max of 4.** The fix list asked for a max of 4 steps. That was the reviewer's figure from a different measurement: the drawn integer heading against the authority's, with an input that never turns the Cutter's crew Helm. On this run the max is 42.7 at 100 ms and 78.2 at 200 ms. **The owner should decide the max**, after heading smoothing (§3.8).
- **The Weapons seat.** It carries the partner's steering forward until the next publication, so its corrections are much larger: 43 to 121 steps, which is 60° to 170°. That is far above the reviewer's 12–23. G4 prints these figures and does not assert them. They are the case for §3.8 smoothing.
