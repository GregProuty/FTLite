## 4. Deviations from the plan or spec

1. **The mapping offset.** After each confirm the mapping offset is `target − newest local tick` (plan deviation 1). With it, `tick()`'s own-entry steps and the verify mapping agree under the 2-tick slack.
2. **Log entries leave only by acknowledgement.** That means at or before the anchor, or by `PREDICT_LOG_MAX` (plan deviation 2). The spec's §3.4 step 1 trim drops entries before their held watermark returns, so it never builds a lead. This was found in plan review (R1-F1, P0).
3. **Reseat corrections are counted apart from G4's half-tile bound.** A correction that flips this seat's seated state (`correction.reseated`) is a refused action taken back, as §3.7 allows. The cause: two held frames latched in one authority tick, so the authority walked one step fewer, and the predicted sit was refused as out of reach. The character then snaps back about 1.8 tiles. The rule: at most 3 per seat in the 60 s run, at most 2 tiles each. The spec's §6 G4 row now says so.
4. **`gunManualNow`, a one-pump value in the session.** R plus the autocannon trigger in one pump fires on that tick. The sim applies `gunMode` before the gun fires in the same tick.
5. **A test that now expects the opposite.** The crew Helm test "A does not clear the held heading" now expects A to clear it. That matches the sim and the owner's 2026-11-19 A/D-turn decision.
6. **Fire crackle reads the confirmed interior.** It lags by the lead. Fires come from enemy hits, so a prediction would not show them earlier.
7. **`helmVent?` is gone from `DuelViewSource`.** No source provided it any more.
8. **§3.8 is built.** The spec deferred correction smoothing ("not smoothed, yet"). The owner ordered it on 2026-11-20, and it is now built: see §7, Smoothing.

### Smooth 60 fps (Fable #7, #8)

The owner asked for smooth 60 fps with no stuttering asteroids. The cause and the fix:

- **Frame timing.** The view's alpha was measured from the nominal tick boundary, but local ticks ran only when the late `setTimeout` pump fired. A frame drawn between the boundary and the pump clamped at alpha 1 (a frozen frame), and the next frame jumped. Now every drawn frame first runs the local ticks due, through the pump's own tick loop (`runLocalTicks`, one input sample per call as before). So alpha stays in [0, 1] without the clamp.
- **Rocks.** Online, while predicting, the rock track blends over one tick, as the ships do. Before, it used the ticks since the last frame, which was 2 after a late pump.
  - While P is held at the cap, alpha is 1, so the rocks hold with the ships.
  - A backward tick of at most 30 ticks (a retarget) re-syncs the positions and keeps the trails. A longer one is still a new history.
  - Single player passes no gap and is unchanged.

## 7. Smoothing (§3.8, built)

The owner ordered correction smoothing on 2026-11-20. Re-simulation replaces P at once, so a correction was a jump on screen. The figures, from §6: own heading corrections up to 43 steps at 100 ms, 43 to 121 steps at the Weapons seat when the pilot turns, and retargets that move the whole predicted world.

### How it works

Drawing only. The sim, P and the predictor's rules do not change.

- **The smoother** (`render/duel/correctionSmoother.ts`). It is pure and takes the time from the caller.
  - Each ship and missile keeps a visual offset: shown position minus new position. It decays linearly to zero over `SMOOTH_MS = 100`.
  - A second correction inside 100 ms adds to what is still shown, so it does not jump.
  - The heading offset wraps the short way.
  - These bodies jump, as before: a missile that appeared or ended, and a body whose offset is over `SMOOTH_MAX_U = 200` u (a respawn or teleport).
- **The session** takes P's bodies (`predictedBodiesOf`, now exported from the predictor) before each confirm. After a `'resimulated'` result it shifts the smoother, which covers corrections and retargets alike. A reset, a dropped predictor, a link close and a re-attach clear it.
- **The drawing.** `PrevBody` has optional `ox, oy, oh`, which `interpolatedShip` and `interpolatedMissile` add after the blend. While predicting, the online source's `prev` and `missilePrev` carry the current offsets. With the fields absent the result is unchanged, so Single player and the confirmed view are unchanged.

### What is smoothed

Every ship and missile drawn, picked or followed through `interpolatedShip`, `interpolatedMissile` or `followTarget`:

- the map's ships, rings, contact marks, picks and edge arrows;
- the Weapons map's markers, turret muzzles, the lead pip and the gun sight;
- missile trails and the missile camera;
- the scene's aims, steers, pings and hover readout;
- the cameras' follow.

### What is not smoothed

- **Effects placed by `placeEvents`** (beams, sparks, gun and pew hits). They stay at P's positions. They are brief.
- **Rocks**, at first. They are never mispredicted, but a retarget moves their draw tick. They are smoothed since, through a draw-tick offset (§8).
- **The own character.** Its corrections are at most 0.19 tile.
- **Reseats.**
- **Reads straight from P's state, all cosmetic or logic:**
  - the turret bracket test, a logic check on the integer heading;
  - the sit zoom (`frameZoom`), camera framing;
  - the sound pan;
  - the fog blip, which is the enemy's.

### Routed through the offsets (follow-up)

These draw relative to the own ship or the own torpedo, so they now carry the offsets. None of them changes without an offset, so Single player is unchanged.

- **The Helm's predicted flight path and its contact mark.** The path is rotated by `oh` about P's own ship, then shifted by `(ox, oy)`, so it starts at the smoothed marker and turns with it.
- **The own torpedo's edge arrow.** `TacticalModelInput` gains an optional `missilePrev`; the scene already passed it. The arrow is shifted by the torpedo's offset.
- **The MANUAL laser aim line.** It is now clamped about the drawn nose: P's heading plus `oh`.

### The rock jump after a sit (diagnosed and smoothed)

The probe's one real stutter: rocks jumped about 3 ticks (50 ms, about 2 u) in one frame, right after a seat change, never in flight.

- **Not the sit.** Headless, on the real authority and stream (`onlineDuel` + `predictedSeat`, 0/30/100 ms round trips, a sit or stand every second), a sit never retargets: it verifies, or re-simulates on the same tick (`reseated`). A sit makes no publication off the cadence (only controls and attaches publish immediately), and the control revision moves only on pause controls, so no reset follows a sit. The stall below reproduces the jump with no sit and with every entry producing.
- **A main-thread stall.** In every recorded host-helm phase the host window's first frame interval is 92 to 112 ms: the probe's own hook install, right after the host's sit is applied. The guest windows' first intervals are 4 ms. So the jump follows a 100 ms stall of the predicting window.
- **Why a stall moves P about 3 ticks.** During the stall the session sends no held frames, and the publications that acknowledged the last frame sent before it are replaced before the session sees them. The first publication confirmed after the stall acknowledges that old frame, and the anchor takes it as latched at the newest tick. The measured target then counts the stall as round trip, and the up to 4 catch-up ticks (`LOCAL_TICKS_MAX`) step P on past the time that passed. P lands about 3 ticks ahead. The next anchors (fresh frames) pull it back 3 ticks, or leave it inside the 2-tick slack. Reproduced in `tests/onlineDuelSession.test.ts` ("a 100 ms main-thread stall"): raw P steps −2 ticks in a frame 50 ms after the stall (lag 0). Running the due local ticks before the confirm does not help (the anchor is still the stale frame), so the predictor is unchanged. This is a legitimate retarget after a stall, and it is smoothed.
- **The smoothing.** On each re-simulation the session also records a draw-tick offset: P's tick before minus after. It decays linearly to 0 over `SMOOTH_MS`, adds to what is still shown, and is cleared on a reset. The online source passes it to the scene's rock track, which adds it to the rail and spin draw tick (`rockDrawTick`). A free rock is blended from the track's last position, so it gets the same offset along its blend. On a tick change other than the given one-tick gap (a retarget, or two ticks in one frame), its last position is put one gap back along its step, so the blend stays one tick wide, as the rails' does. Single player and the guest's replica pass no gap and no offset, so their path is unchanged.
