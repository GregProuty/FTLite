# Roadmap: the next ten features

Written 2026-11-10 when the owner asked for an autonomous run through the feature list ("take the recommendation and move on"). Each feature goes through the same loop: brainstorm (decisions taken on the recommended option and recorded in the spec), spec, Codex spec review, plan, Codex plan review, subagent-driven execution with per-task reviews, Opus final review, fast-forward merge, Codex implementation review. The foundation spec's build order (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-foundation-design.md` §11) covers the first four; the sub-projects in §1 and the open items in §14 give the rest, decomposed so each is playable on its own.

| # | Feature | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **3-i Skills and XP:** six skills with XP and levels from a threshold table, productive predicates per station, XP from repairs, skill effects on every station formula, repair speed, cooling per unit and sealed doors, Engineering bonus bars with power shedding | foundation §5 Skill model, §11 item 3 | merged 2026-11-10 |
| 2 | **3-ii Characters:** the four pre-made characters with an Asset, a Complication and a skill head start; lobby character display; game-over skill gains | foundation §5 Crew, §7 session flow, §11 item 3 | merged 2026-11-10 |
| 3 | **4-i Director:** stress and pacing phase machine, elapsed-time escalation, concurrency cap, routing hazards to idle consoles, player-count scaling, replacing the timer spawner | foundation §5 Director, §11 item 4 | merged 2026-11-10 |
| 4 | **4-ii Telemetry and acceptance:** session telemetry log (time to first death, stations never manned, longest idle per player, how the run ended), game-over summary, long headless soak test for the director | foundation §10, §11 item 4 | merged 2026-11-11 |
| 5 | **Combat-i Enemy ship:** an abstract enemy ship with hull and weapons that fires at our rooms on the director's cadence; our beams and torpedoes can target it; the encounter ends when it is destroyed or withdraws | sub-project 2 | merged 2026-11-11 |

| 16 | **Demo-vi Encounters and fire retune:** each hazard node is one fight with a contact notice, a win that repairs 8 hull and fast-charges the drive; quiet event and shop nodes; slower fire spread, an ambient fire cap of two, enemy hits start the fires | sub-project 4 | merged (manual two-player check open) |

| 18 | **Demo-viii Asteroid fields and the enemy window:** asteroid-field nodes (a minute of weaker, breach-first rocks, cleared for scrap and a fast drive); an FTL-style enemy ship window in the right column with rooms, glyphs, shields, hull pips, the charge bar, a reticle, enemy crew, fires and breaches our hits start (they burn its hull and systems), our bolts and the enemy shots drawn between the ships; the map replaces it at Navigation | sub-project 4 | in review (manual two-player check open) |
| 19 | **Demo-ix Boarders and scrap:** boarders suffocate and burn like the crew (6 per second below 10 % oxygen, 3 in a fire), so venting or letting a room burn kills them for the usual scrap with no XP; every scrap reward grows with the column (per jump 10 + 4c, enemy 20 + 6c, surrender 15 + 4c, boarder 8 + 2c, field 15 + 5c) and the notices quote what they pay | sub-project 4 | in review (manual check open) |
| 20 | **Coop-i Working together:** the enemy re-tunes its shields every 20 s, so Science locks the frequency for Weapons (8 s, any selector tuned, +0.15 to hit); two or more crew on one fire, breach, failed console or revive each work 1.25 times faster (2.5 times one person for two) and reviving is shared; a callout button (R, /, Y) shows what you need over your head and flashes the station's Engineering row; downed crew call for help; the end of a run counts locked shots, shared repair seconds and revives | user request 2026-11-12 | in review (manual two-player check open) |
| 21 | **Coop-ii Working together, part two:** the pilot's evasive manoeuvre doubles the dodge chance (capped at 80 %) for one second on a teammate's "now!", then 6 s to recharge; on the cutter torpedoes are carried by hand from a magazine in the Lower Hold to the Weapons console (a carrier is slower and repairs nothing; downed, the torpedo goes back); every conscious crew member votes on an event (standing Confirm; majority, the pilot breaks ties, 15 s at most); a jump needs a second crew member's Confirm within 5 s unless the pilot is alone | user request 2026-11-12 | in review (manual two-player check open) |
| 22 | **Loop-i Sectors, fuel and the Pursuit:** a run is three sectors of six-column beacon maps with 2 to 4 beacons a column and sideways routes; every jump costs fuel (start 8; kills, cleared fields and shops give more; with none, wait for help); a pursuing fleet front advances with every jump and wait and overruns the beacons behind it (an elite fight, no content); each sector starts at a safe beacon and every other beacon is hidden (`?`) until visited, except shops and the exit; escaping the third sector wins | user request 2026-11-12 (FTL's route loop, a peaceful start and random encounters) | in review (manual check open: does the route choice feel like FTL?) |
| 23 | **Loop-ii Upgrades:** scrap buys system levels (up to three per system, 30 / 45 / 60) and reactor bars (up to five, 25 to 65) at any time from an Upgrades page on the Engineering console; a new bar arrives unpowered; shops keep hull, torpedoes and fuel | user request 2026-11-12 (FTL's loop: scrap is mostly spent on upgrades, prices rising) | in review (manual check open: is spending scrap a real decision?) |

Deferred beyond this list: networking (sub-project 5), a solo drone, the shared luck resource, procedural ships.

Each feature's spec and plan live in `docs/superpowers/specs/` and `docs/superpowers/plans/` with the date prefix; gates are appended to `docs/gates.md`; the execution ledger is `.superpowers/sdd/progress.md`.
