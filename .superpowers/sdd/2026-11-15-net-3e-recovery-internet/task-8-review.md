## Important — fix before accepting Task 8

The recovery report and candidate both record `out/main/hostProcess.js`, `out/main/transportWindow.js`, other main chunks and renderer JavaScript, but none of those hashes is compared. A read-only in-memory probe changed only `localRecovery.report.source.artifactHashes['out/main/hostProcess.js']` to a different valid SHA-256 in the retained readiness report; admission still succeeded. Thus the new runtime-binding fix does not establish that the candidate whose readiness is reported ran the recovery code. Compare the complete required executable runtime set and reject missing/changed entries; preserve historical revision reuse only with explicit byte-equality provenance. Add negative cases for hostProcess and a renderer bundle, not only index.js (`tests/internetAppReport.test.ts:41`).

Line 71 compares the two devices' origins only to each other; line 81 binds deployedService's attestation only to its own run; line 83 compares other runs' identities but never their origin to that deployment. A focused in-memory fixture based on the retained native report was accepted with deployedService at `https://deployed.example` and both forcedRelayGameplay and twoMachineGameplay at `https://other.example`, with internetReady true. This can occur through the launcher without changing runtime bytes: `candidate-manifest.ts:27` keeps serviceOrigin outside identity, and `internet-app-smoke.ts:84` trusts that manifest field when selecting its development-mode origin. Bind the intended canonical service origin into candidate identity and require every external gate to match the deployment attestation and that identity. Add cross-gate and manifest-origin mismatch tests.
