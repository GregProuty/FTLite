# Open-licence top-down crew sprites for FTLite

Scope: top-down (not side-view platformer) human character sprite sheets and character generators, 16-32 px, suitable as a scrappy civilian spaceship crew with 4+ direction walk, downed/dead and sit/interact poses, and friendly to per-player tinting. Licence checked on the primary page in every case unless flagged otherwise. Research date: 2026-11-09.

Method note: OpenGameArt blocks the normal fetcher (HTTP 403) so OGA pages were pulled with a browser user-agent and parsed locally; the licence field, author and description quoted below are from the live OGA page HTML. Kenney's own site description pages are sparse (they list only licence, tile size and file count), so some Kenney details are marked as gaps.

## Key Question 1: Which CC0/CC-BY top-down human character packs exist at 16-32 px with walk cycles in 4+ directions?

### Takeaway
There is no single CC0 pack that is simultaneously top-down, sci-fi-civilian, 16-32 px, 4-direction, and complete with down/sit poses. The strongest CC0 candidates are Ninja Adventure (Pixel-Boy & AAA; 50+ 16x16 characters, 4-way, includes robots), Puny Characters (Shade; 16x16, 8-directional, has Hurt + Death), and Zelda-like tilesets and sprites (ArMM1998; 16x16 incl. a character template). The best CC-BY fits are Antifarea's Twelve 16x18 RPG sprites plus base (CC-BY 3.0, includes townspeople and a base body) and the brand-new Cozy Space Station (CC-BY 4.0, 8 sci-fi crew characters, 16 px grid, but animation status unconfirmed).

### Inferences
- For a 32 px engine tile with ~0.6-tile crew collision, 16x16 base characters rendered at 2x (Ninja Adventure, Puny Characters, Zelda-like, 15 Top-Down) are the natural fit; Antifarea's 16x18 and isaiah658's taller-than-16 characters also scale cleanly at 2x into a 32x32/32x48 slot.
- None of the CC0 packs is explicitly "civilian sci-fi"; Ninja Adventure's robots and the plain-clothes humans in Antifarea, isaiah658 and Ninja Adventure are the closest to a Firefly-style crew. Cozy Space Station is the only open-licence set that is literally sci-fi crew, but it is CC-BY and probably static (see Gaps).
- Puny Characters' 8-directional sheets are a superset of what FTLite needs (free analog movement maps well to 8 directions), and its 32x32 canvas with 16x16 footprint leaves room for weapons/tools without clipping.

### Gaps
- Kenney Roguelike Characters: sprite size, whether sprites are animated and whether layers (body/clothing/hair) exist are not stated on kenney.nl, and the itch.io mirror URL returned 404. From general knowledge this pack is 16x16 single-frame layered parts (no walk cycle), but that could not be verified on the primary page during this research — treat as unverified.
- Ninja Adventure: the itch page does not enumerate per-character animations; the 4-direction claim comes from a user comment the author did not dispute. The downloaded zip should be inspected to confirm Walk/Idle/Dead frames.
- Zelda-like (ArMM1998): the page does not state the number of walk directions or frames for the character; only "character with sword animation and character template" is confirmed.
- Cozy Space Station: whether the 8 crew characters have walk cycles at all is not stated ("39 transparent sprites" suggests static), and the itch.io licence page was not fetched to confirm it matches the OGA CC-BY 4.0 statement.
- 15 Top-Down Character Sprites: frame count per walk not given.
- No reliable OGA search results surfaced for CC0 "sci-fi character top-down" — the OGA keyword search returned mostly side-view shooters, GUI packs and the Top-Down Sci-fi Shooter packs (which are vector/armed soldiers, not civilian pixel crew). Those pages were not fetched individually.

## Key Question 2: Which include a prone/downed/dead frame and a sitting or interacting pose, or are easy to extend?

### Takeaway
Among small (16 px) open packs only Puny Characters (CC0) ships both Hurt and Death animations; Tiny 16 (CC-BY 4.0) has a separate dead.png with dead and sleeping characters; RunninBlood (restricted licence) has Death plus a "Pulling" interaction. Sitting poses at 16 px essentially do not exist in open packs; at 64 px, LPC Revised and LPC Expanded supply sit, run, jump, idle and emotes under CC-BY-SA 3.0.

### Cited Findings
- Puny Characters (CC0, 16x16, 8-dir): Hurt Animation and Death Animation listed; also Throw (usable as an interact/repair gesture); no sit — [Puny Characters (OGA)](https://opengameart.org/content/puny-characters); [itch page](https://merchant-shade.itch.io/16x16-puny-characters)
- Tiny 16: Basic (CC-BY 4.0): dead.png file; "a few dead and sleeping characters" added; 2-frame animations only — [Tiny 16: Basic (OGA)](https://opengameart.org/content/tiny-16-basic)
- RunninBlood 16x16 (custom, no redistribution): Death, Pitfall, and "Pulling (for picking up items or pulling objects)" — [RunninBlood (itch.io)](https://runninblood.itch.io/16x16-top-down-character)
- Antifarea base: the pack includes a base template body; a community-made attack pose for the base exists ("attack-animation-for-16x18-rpg-base-sprites"), demonstrating the base is extendable — [Antifarea (OGA)](https://opengameart.org/content/twelve-16x18-rpg-sprites-plus-base)
- Zelda-like (CC0): includes "character tamplet" (template) explicitly for drawing new poses — [Zelda-like (OGA)](https://opengameart.org/content/zelda-like-tilesets-and-sprites)
- LPC Revised Character Basics (CC-BY-SA 3.0, 64x64): "4 animations: animated idle, walk, run, and jump. 6 still frames for sitting and emoting poses"; "Masculine and Feminine bodies in 11 skin tones"; "17 clothing options for both body types with 44 colors each"; includes "Body guidelines to help content creators animate new content"; credits: Eliza Wyatt, Stephen Challener (Redshrike), Durrani, BenCreating, Johannes Sjölund (Wulax), Thecilekli, BlueCarrot16 — [[LPC Revised] Character Basics! (OGA)](https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-revised-character-basics)
- LPC Expanded has a dedicated "[LPC Expanded] Sit, Run, Jump, & More" submission — [OGA search result](https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-expanded-sit-run-jump-more) (not fetched; licence assumed LPC CC-BY-SA/GPL but unverified)
- Original LPC base animations are spellcast, thrust, walk, slash, shoot and hurt (hurt = the fall-down/dead sequence); LPC Expanded adds bow, climb, run, jump — [ULPC generator README](https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator)
- Ninja Adventure: author says newer characters have a jump animation; death/sit not stated on page — [Ninja Adventure (itch.io)](https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack)

### Inferences
- A "downed" pose can be derived from Puny Characters' Death frames (final frame as a static prone sprite) with zero licence friction (CC0). For packs without a death frame (Antifarea, 15 Top-Down, isaiah658), the simplest extension is a hand-drawn prone frame per base body — at 16 px this is a few dozen pixels of work and the CC0/CC-BY licences permit derivative work.
- A "sit at console" pose at 16 px can usually be faked by cropping the lower body behind the console sprite and using the idle-down/idle-up frame; no open 16 px pack provides a true sit frame, so plan to draw one.
- Per-player tinting: all CC0 packs above permit modification; Puny Characters' author confirmed no fixed palette exists, so a shader-based hue shift or a recolour of a single "uniform" colour band (as ATMANAN's six colour variants demonstrate) is the practical route.

### Gaps
- No open-licence 16-32 px pack with a dedicated sitting frame was found.
- Frame counts for Puny Characters' Death/Hurt were not stated; a commenter reports idle/walk at 4-5 frames per character.
- The "[LPC Expanded] Sit, Run, Jump & More" page was not fetched; its exact licence and author list are unverified.

## Key Question 3: Which open character generators produce top-down sprites, and what are their licence implications (LPC confirmed CC-BY-SA/GPL)?

### Takeaway
The Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator is the only mature open generator; its output is 64x64, 4-direction, and must be credited to every contributing author, with the most restrictive combined licence being CC-BY-SA 4.0+/GPL 3.0+ (some parts OGA-BY, CC-BY or CC0). No CC0 open-source 16 px top-down generator was found; Kenney's Roguelike Characters (CC0 layered parts) is the closest "mix-and-match" alternative but is not a generator tool.

### Cited Findings
- ULPC README lists five licences across its assets: "CC0: Allowed to be used under any circumstances, attribution not required"; "CC-BY-SA: Credit the authors, don't encrypt or protect the work, and release derivatives under CC-BY-SA 4.0 or later" (described as the most restrictive licence in the generator); "CC-BY: Credit the authors, and don't encrypt or protect the work"; "OGA-BY: Credit the authors. Encryption is allowed, so DRM-protected games are permitted"; "GPL: Release derivative artwork under GPL 3.0 or later" — [ULPC GitHub](https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator)
- Attribution rule: "you must at least credit all the authors (except for CC0 licensed artwork)"; comply by distributing the full CREDITS.csv or a list of credits for the assets used; credits "must be easy for users to find, for example on a credits screen or through a visible link" — [ULPC GitHub](https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator)
- Generator tooling: downloadable credits sheet (CSV or plain text) listing licence info for the selected assets; CREDITS.csv in the repo lists authors, licences and source URLs for every image; FAQ recommends exporting JSON alongside the PNG to recover credits later — [ULPC README raw](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator/master/README.md)
- Frame size and bases: original LPC "stuck to a standard 64x64 format"; weapons use oversize frames (ULPC addition); bases are adult male/female, LPC Expanded adds child and elderly (many assets not drawn for them), LPC Revised uses smaller heads — [ULPC README raw](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator/master/README.md)
- The live generator site states "Free/Libre pixel art from the Liberated Pixel Cup" and "You must credit the authors" — [ULPC live site](https://liberatedpixelcup.github.io/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator/)
- The original LPC Base Assets on OGA are licensed CC-BY-SA 3.0 with note: "The assets by Leana 'Sharm' Zimmerman and Stephen 'Redshrike' Challener are also available under the OGA-BY 3.0 License"; "Images are copyright their respective authors, as listed in CREDITS.TXT" — [LPC Base Assets (OGA)](https://opengameart.org/content/liberated-pixel-cup-lpc-base-assets-sprites-map-tiles)
- LPC Revised Character Basics (an alternative LPC-compatible base with idle/sit/run/jump) is CC-BY-SA 3.0 and lives in Eliza Wyatt's GitHub repository — [[LPC Revised] Character Basics! (OGA)](https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-revised-character-basics)
- Non-open generators found: "Tiny Pixel Fantasy – Character Generator" (cazwolf, itch.io) is royalty-free under a custom EULA, 16x16 directional sprites, not open source — [Tiny Pixel Fantasy Generator](https://cazwolf.itch.io/tiny-pixel-fantasy-generator); "Pixel Character Creator v1.1" (mounirtohami) is a $5 desktop tool exporting 24x24 sprites with platform/top-down modes — [Pixel Character Creator](https://mounirtohami.itch.io/pixeldesigner); a web search for an open-source CC0 top-down generator on GitHub returned no such project — [search result summary](https://opengameart.org/content/base-character-spritesheet-16x16)

### Inferences
- Using ULPC output in FTLite means: (a) a visible credits screen listing every contributing artist, (b) the resulting sprite sheets must be redistributable under CC-BY-SA 4.0+ (and/or GPL 3.0+) — i.e. the art cannot be locked behind encryption/DRM, and anyone may reuse the modified sheets; this does not force the game code under GPL but the artwork files must carry the licence. Selecting only CC0/OGA-BY parts in the generator is possible but must be verified per-asset via the exported credits sheet.
- ULPC's 64x64 frames are 2x the target density of a 32 px tile game: a 64 px character would stand two tiles tall. Downscaling pixel art 50% destroys outlines, so ULPC is a poor pixel-density match for FTLite unless the whole tileset moves to 64 px tiles (LPC tiles are 32 px with 64 px characters, roughly 1.5-2 tiles tall).
- Kenney Roguelike Characters' CC0 layered parts are the only "generator-like" CC0 route at 16 px, but without walk cycles (unverified, see Gaps) they would need hand-animated bodies.

### Gaps
- The exact list of animations supported in the current ULPC web UI (e.g. idle, sit, emote, combat idle, backslash, halfslash, climb, watering) could not be read: the live page is JS-rendered and the sanderfrenken.github.io mirror exceeded the fetch size limit; only the README's animation names (spellcast, thrust, walk, slash, shoot, hurt, bow, climb, run, jump) are confirmed.
- Whether the ULPC UI offers a licence filter (e.g. "CC0 only") was not confirmed; the README describes only a credits-sheet export.

## Key Question 4: Which packs style-match common 16/32 px sci-fi tilesets (same pixel density, outline style)?

### Takeaway
Pixel-density match is straightforward for the 16 px packs (Ninja Adventure, Puny Characters, Zelda-like, Antifarea, isaiah658, 15 Top-Down) against 16 px sci-fi tilesets such as Buch's CC0 "Sci-fi Interior tiles"; Cozy Space Station is the only character set drawn on the same grid and palette as a matching sci-fi furniture/workstation set. Outline-style matching (dark outline vs. no outline) could not be verified from page text alone and needs a visual check.

### Cited Findings
- Buch, "Sci-fi Interior tiles" — OGA License(s): CC0; tags scifi, tileset, interior; credit "NOT REQUIRED, though appreciated: credit me as Buch" — [Sci-fi Interior tiles (OGA)](https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-interior-tiles)
- Hyptosis, "Space Scifi Rpg Tiles 48x48" — OGA License(s): CC0; tags jrpg, rpg maker, scifi, space station, spaceship; 48x48 (not 16/32) — [Space Scifi Rpg Tiles 48x48 (OGA)](https://opengameart.org/node/80030)
- Jesse McCarthy, "Space Station Tileset" — OGA License(s): CC-BY 3.0; tagged Platformer / sidescroller (side-view, not a top-down match) — [Space Station Tileset (OGA)](https://opengameart.org/content/space-station-tileset)
- Cozy Space Station — "every pack shares one palette and one 16 px grid, so they fit together"; includes workstations and tools alongside the 8 crew characters, i.e. characters and consoles are already style-matched — [Cozy Space Station (OGA)](https://opengameart.org/node/194876)
- Antifarea's sprites "are intended to work with the 16x16 town tileset" on OGA — a 16 px grid reference — [Antifarea (OGA)](https://opengameart.org/content/twelve-16x18-rpg-sprites-plus-base)
- Tiny 16 uses DawnBringer's 16-colour palette — a constraint that eases palette-matching to other DB16 sci-fi tiles — [Tiny 16: Basic (OGA)](https://opengameart.org/content/tiny-16-basic)
- Puny Characters is tagged Isometric, hexagonal, orthogonal and Top-down and a commenter calls it "one of the few isometric-compatible resources", implying a fairly neutral 3/4 perspective that fits most top-down tilesets — [Puny Characters (OGA)](https://opengameart.org/content/puny-characters)

### Inferences
- The cleanest pairing for a 32 px engine tile is a 16 px character pack and a 16 px sci-fi tileset both scaled 2x (e.g. Puny Characters or Ninja Adventure + Buch Sci-fi Interior tiles), giving one pixel density across the scene; mixing a native-32 px tileset with 2x-scaled 16 px characters would produce a visible "chunky character on fine floor" mismatch.
- Scrappy civilian tone: Antifarea's townsperson/man/woman sprites, isaiah658's modern adults/children and Ninja Adventure's humans read as plain-clothes; Puny Characters' soldier/archer/wizard classes are fantasy and would need clothing recolours or edits to read as spaceship crew.

### Gaps
- Outline style (black outline vs selective outline vs none) for each pack could not be read from page text; preview images were not analysed. A side-by-side visual check of each candidate against the chosen tileset is required.
- No 32 px-native, open-licence top-down civilian sci-fi character pack with 4-direction walks was found in this pass; the 32 px sci-fi character results were all paid (GameDev Market / Cubebrush).
