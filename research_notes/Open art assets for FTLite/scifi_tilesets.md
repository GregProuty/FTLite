# Open-licence top-down sci-fi spaceship interior tilesets for FTLite (32 px engine tile; 16 px @2x OK)

Research date: 2026-11-09. All licences below were read on the primary asset page (OpenGameArt pages fetched directly; itch.io pages fetched directly; GitHub README/LICENSE files fetched raw). "Verified" means the licence field/text was seen on that page; "stated in comments only" is flagged as weaker.

Quick verdict for FTLite: there is no single CC0/CC-BY 16 or 32 px pack that covers *everything* (walls + floors + animated doors + consoles + breach/damage decals) in a scrappy style. The strongest base candidates are (1) Buch's **Sci-fi Interior tiles** (CC0, 16 px, cartoon-clean), (2) MurphysDad/HaywardMorihara **Sci-Fi Facility Asset Pack** (CC0, includes walls/floors/animated computers/gates), (3) Spaceyyy **Cozy Space Interiors** (CC-BY 4.0 copy on OGA; 16 px; has closed/open sliding door + 4-frame animation; shares a palette with 15 sibling packs), and (4) Hyptosis **Space Scifi Rpg Tiles 48x48** (CC0, most "lived-in"/RPG look but 48 px). Damage/fire/smoke must come from separate CC0 VFX packs (Reactorcore, Kenney, Limofeus). Nothing found with true hull-breach/wall-damage decals under an open licence.

## Key Question 1: Which CC0/CC-BY sci-fi interior tilesets exist on OpenGameArt, itch.io, Kenney.nl, and GitHub at 16 or 32 px?

### Takeaway
About six genuinely open (CC0 or CC-BY) top-down sci-fi interior sets exist; most are small and 16 px. Kenney has no sci-fi *interior* tileset (only CC0 RTS/exterior, roguelike, 1-bit and VFX packs). GitHub open-source games (SS13, SS14) have huge 32 px top-down station art but under CC-BY-SA 3.0 (copyleft) with some NC assets.

### Inferences
- Buch's 448x192 sheet and Nikles's 512x256 autotile sheet are both 16 px grids (dimensions divide evenly by 16, not by 32 in the 448 case); at 2x they match FTLite's 32 px tile.
- Hyptosis 48x48 is the only CC0 set with a genuinely "RPG spaceship/colony" scope (tags: colony, mars, alien, spaceship), but 48 px does not scale cleanly to 32 px; it would need downsampling or using 48 px tiles at a 1.5-tile-per-cell ratio.
- Kenney's Sci-Fi RTS is an exterior/base-building set (tags building, rts, map); it does not supply interior walls or doors.
- Cozy Space is the only *family* of open packs sharing a palette and grid (interiors + props + furniture + crew + FX) — but only the Interiors pack is confirmed CC-BY 4.0 (via the OGA upload); the other packs on itch are under the "no redistribution" house licence.

### Gaps
- Tile size is not stated on the Aske4, SteelSoldier, MurphysDad, or Kenney Sci-Fi RTS pages; only inferable from file dimensions (not downloaded for those).
- Absolute publish dates are missing on most itch.io pages (only relative "x ago"); OGA dates are exact.
- No GitHub repository was found that hosts an original CC0 top-down spaceship *interior* tileset; search surfaced only itch/OGA listings.

## Key Question 2: Which include doors with open/closed frames and wall-damage/breach decals?

### Takeaway
Open-licence animated doors exist in Cozy Space Interiors (CC-BY 4.0: closed, open, 4-frame animation) and in Sci-Fi Facility (CC0: animated "electric gates"). No open-licence pack found contains hull-breach or wall-damage decals; the only "damaged floor" tile found is in TheRealFanMan's non-CC pack, and fire/smoke must come from separate CC0 VFX packs.

### Cited Findings
- Cozy Space Interiors: "sliding door in closed and open states, plus a 4-frame opening animation"; front walls include vent, pipes, screen, wall light; "damage tiles are not mentioned". — [aquumgifts itch](https://aquumgifts.itch.io/cozy-space-interiors)
- Sci-Fi Facility Asset Pack: "Animated object sprites (including computers, noisemakers, electric gates, glowing orbs, buttons)" — gates, not conventional doors. — [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-facility-asset-pack)
- TheRealFanMan Sci-Fi Interior: door "3 states (closed, semi-open, open)", floors "clean, damaged, pressure plate, bloody" — but licence forbids redistribution and is not CC. — [TheRealFanMan itch](https://therealfanman.itch.io/top-down-sci-fi-interior)
- DithArt Dark Organic Spaceship (paid, 12 EUR): doors "Vertical and horizontal, each with closed and open states", pipes, 16 front-wall variants. — [DithArt itch](https://dithart.itch.io/ditharts-sci-fi-tileset-5-darkorganicspaceship)
- Winlu (paid, $20+): doors labelled "ENGINE" and "CARGO" visible in previews; pipes, crates, barrels, consoles. — [WinLu itch](https://winlu.itch.io/spaceship-tileset)
- Fire/smoke, CC0: **Fire Smoke Animations** by Reactorcore, OGA, 28 Jul 2025, CC0: "Pixel-art sprites pack depicting fire and smoke frame animations. Includes multiple variants, styles and sizes... both sheets and individual images... PNGs with transparent background." — [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/fire-smoke-animations)
- Fire/gas-leak, CC0: Limofeus **Pixel simulations** — explosions, flames, gas leak, gas explosion at 64x64 and 16x16. — [Limofeus itch](https://limofeus.itch.io/pixel-simulations)
- Smoke, CC0: Kenney **Smoke Particles** (70 files) and **Particle Pack** (80 files, 512 px). — [Kenney Smoke Particles](https://kenney.nl/assets/smoke-particles); [Kenney Particle Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack)
- SS13/SS14 (CC-BY-SA 3.0) contain breach/damage/fire overlays as part of the full game sprite set (by design of the games), but the licence is share-alike and SS14 mixes in NC assets. — [tgstation README](https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/blob/master/README.md); [SS14 README](https://github.com/space-wizards/space-station-14/blob/master/README.md)

### Inferences
- Breach/wall-damage decals will almost certainly need to be custom-drawn (or derived from CC0 floor/wall tiles) — none exist under CC0/CC-BY among the packs surveyed.
- Reactorcore's fire/smoke pack (2025, CC0, multiple sizes) is the best drop-in for FTLite's fire/smoke layer; Limofeus's 16 px gas-leak animation suits a breach-venting effect at 2x.

### Gaps
- Exact frame sizes in Reactorcore's pack were not extracted (zip not downloaded).
- Whether Sci-Fi Facility's "electric gates" read as doors in a ship context is unverified (no preview analysed).

## Key Question 3: Which have a consistent palette suitable for a scrappy "Firefly" ship rather than clean military sci-fi?

### Takeaway
Hyptosis's 48x48 CC0 set (colony/mars/station/alien tags, hand-drawn RPG look) is closest to a lived-in, non-military feel; Buch's set is self-described "too cartoonish" (clean, bright); Cozy Space is deliberately "cozy" and clean with a strict 110-colour master palette. Nothing open-licence is explicitly "junker/scrappy" — coldrice's paid 16x16 set is the only one that advertises "junkers".

### Cited Findings
- Buch: "the committer found them too cartoonish for their purposes." — [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-interior-tiles)
- Hyptosis 48x48 tags: "jrpg RPG rpg maker scifi science space station spacestation colony mars alien spaceship ship"; author: "won't work with some of [my other sets]". — [OpenGameArt node/80030](https://opengameart.org/node/80030)
- LPC goes to space (CC-BY-SA/GPL) explicitly took its palette from Hyptosis's 48x48 set, so the two mix visually but NOT licence-wise (copyleft). — [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-goes-to-space)
- Cozy Space: "every pack shares one palette and one 16 px grid, so they fit together"; Complete bundle includes a "Master palette (110 colours)". — [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/cozy-space-interiors-16-px-top-down-sci-fi-tileset); [Cozy Space Complete](https://aquumgifts.itch.io/cozy-space-complete)
- Sci-Fi Facility is themed "laboratory, facility" with hazmat/spy characters (clean lab feel). — [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-facility-asset-pack)
- coldrice (paid): "clean spaceships, junkers, alien craft, and stranger designs". — [coldrice itch](https://coldrice.itch.io/16x16-sci-fi-tiles)
- exPLAYration's retro station pack used a "four-color palette" (now 404). — [itch forum](https://itch.io/t/1355603/game-asset-topdown-retro-space-station-tileset-and-characters-released)

### Inferences
- Mixing guidance: Buch (16 px) + Nikles autotile (same art) + Sci-Fi Facility (16 px-ish, CC0) are plausible to blend after palette-matching in Aseprite; Cozy Space Interiors (16 px) only mixes cleanly with its own siblings. Hyptosis 48 px and 24 px sets do not mix with the 16 px packs without resampling, and Hyptosis himself warns the 48 px set is incompatible with his other sets.
- A "scrappy" look will most likely come from recolouring a CC0 base (Buch or Facility) toward rust/ochre and layering CC0 grime/fire decals, rather than from any existing pack.

### Gaps
- No pack page describes its palette in terms of "worn/rusty/patched"; palette character was inferred from tags and author descriptions, not from pixel analysis.
