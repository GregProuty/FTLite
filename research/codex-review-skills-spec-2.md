# Codex review: 3-i spec (HUD and tests, 2026-11-10)

1. **Medium — [spec:129](/C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-skills-and-xp-design.md:129): Science’s scanning fixture contradicts §6.** A valid scan target must be warned, so “scanning a valid target with none warned” cannot satisfy the predicate. Moreover, any valid scan already satisfies the warned-object alternative. **Fix:** test warned objects with scanning off, no warned objects with scanning on, and inactive objects with stale warning flags; do not claim independent scanning-branch coverage.

2. **Medium — [spec:129](/C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-skills-and-xp-design.md:129): A full ship does not guarantee zero Life Support delivery.** `oxygenStep` applies breathing before injection, so a living seated operator creates room for injection even when oxygen starts full. **Fix:** use a controlled fixture without oxygen sinks for zero delivery; separately test drained-room delivery, breached-room suppression, and expiry at exactly `HAZARD_EVERY`.

3. **High — [spec:132](/C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-skills-and-xp-design.md:132): The shedding count omits the initial spare bar.** Existing power tests establish allocation 9 and capacity 10. Seating a level-8 engineer gives pool 3; allocating two bars produces usage 11, so standing sheds **one**, not two. **Fix:** spend the original spare first, assert usage 12 before standing, then expect two removals. Keep Doors at one bar to require Medbay next; Doors at two bars loses both before Medbay.

4. **Medium — [spec:143](/C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-skills-and-xp-design.md:143): The manual shedding gate also omits allocation.** Reaching Engineering level 4 increases available capacity, but standing only sheds when usage exceeds base capacity. **Fix:** explicitly allocate the original spare and the bonus bar before standing; then require one shed bar.

5. **Medium — [spec:121](/C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-skills-and-xp-design.md:121): The fourth line overlaps the existing progress bar unless that bar moves.** In `hud.ts:71–74`, text rows use 18 px spacing, while progress sits at local y=48. A fourth row at y=54 intersects that bar. Enlarging the background and moving the console leaves this conflict intact. **Fix:** specify fourth-row y=54 and move progress down 18 px to y=66; verify both are visible during repair/revive.

6. **High — [spec:129](/C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-skills-and-xp-design.md:129): Predicate fixtures do not test production of the timestamps required by §§4 and 6.** Manually setting stamps lets broken Engineering input, remote toggle, or injection stamping pass. Initial `-1` stamps also need explicit coverage: a bare subtraction considers them recent at tick 0. **Fix:** exercise successful and refused bar/coolant changes, remote versus local toggles, actual versus clamped-away injection, initial stamps, and exact window expiry.

7. **Medium — [spec:130–131](/C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-skills-and-xp-design.md:130): Repair coverage does not require all three repair paths or revive exclusions.** §7 applies speed to fire, breach, and power; §5 excludes revive XP. Existing tests exercise baseline repairs and revive timing without skill assertions. **Fix:** parameterize speed and XP tests over all three targets, including two holders finishing on one tick; require unchanged revive duration and zero revive XP at Repair level 10. Use 3.75 s nominal for fire/power and 5 s for breach, with before/after completion assertions.

8. **Medium — [spec:131](/C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-skills-and-xp-design.md:131): Testing `coolingPerUnit` alone misses whether heat consumes its result.** Returning 2 at Engineering level 5 can pass while `heatStep` still uses baseline cooling. **Fix:** add an integrated overdrive test with one coolant unit and nonzero initial heat. Assert unchanged heat across hazard ticks: `2 − 1 × 2 = 0`; after standing, assert a rise of 1 per hazard tick. Nonzero heat exposes excessive cooling hidden by the zero clamp.

9. **High — [spec:132](/C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-skills-and-xp-design.md:132): The property test can miss bonus shedding entirely.** Random inputs, sits, and stands from zero-XP states never establish bonus capacity unless the generator explicitly supplies skilled operators and enough allocation. Downing with allocated bonuses is also absent. **Fix:** generate Engineering levels spanning 3/4 and 7/8, require cases using bonus capacity, and test downing through crew lifecycle. Assert the Engineering floor and the complete shedding priority order.

10. **Medium — [spec:133](/C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-skills-and-xp-design.md:133): Hash coverage omits all three timestamp fields required by §4.** An XP-only hash mutation and a mid-XP continuation can pass even if timestamps are omitted, especially when stamps remain at defaults or their windows expire. **Fix:** round-trip distinct nondefault stamps, mutate each independently and require a hash change, and resume inside active productivity windows. Require version-5 rejection through both the snapshot header and flattened `SIM_VERSION`, matching the existing snapshot test pattern.
