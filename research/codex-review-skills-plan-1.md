3. **P2 — Shortfall fixture actually tests overdrive** ([plan:298](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-10-skills-and-xp.md:298)). Setting Doors to 2 makes it overdriven because its ordinary maximum is 1. Engineering therefore returns true before evaluating bonus bars covering a shortfall; deleting that required predicate would leave this test green. **Fix:** leave Doors at 1 and raise Navigation, Science, and Medbay to 2. This produces usage 12 without overdrive; restore those allocations for the false case.

4. **P2 — No-delivery XP case removes the eligible operator** ([plan:415](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-10-skills-and-xp.md:415)). Making the Life Support operator Dead independently disqualifies them from XP, and the test asserts only the delivery stamp. Assert both `lsDeliveredTick === -1` and Engineering XP remains zero.

5. **P2 — `addXp` can decrease XP** ([plan:193](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-10-skills-and-xp.md:193)). `Math.min(cap, current + amount)` accepts negative amounts and lowers the total, violating §5’s “XP never decreases” contract. The test titled “never decreases” never supplies a negative amount. **Fix:** ignore nonpositive amounts or clamp the increment with `Math.max(0, amount)`; add a negative-increment assertion.
