1. High — **§9: Layout identity does not cover station kinds.** The spec requires adding allocations to `layoutHash`, but station IDs now also determine authoritative behaviour. The existing hash includes only each station’s room and tile, so swapping two valid IDs with equal allocations can change console behaviour while preserving snapshot/replay compatibility. Include the mapped kind in layout identity. 2. High — **§4–5: Offline occupancy has conflicting rules.** Sitting requires an unoccupied station and Conscious player, with no online check; forced standing covers failure only, rather than every transition offline. This permits sitting at an already failed station and remaining seated when Engineering removes its last bar, contrary to the parent’s immediate forced stand on offline rule. The design must explicitly resolve admission at offline consoles and standing on deallocation. [Sitting](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-power-and-consoles-design.md:69), [forced stand](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-power-and-consoles-design.md:79), [parent requirement](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-foundation-design.md:136).

3. High — **§2, §4 and §7: Failure standing is assigned to a step that already ran.** `powerStep` must stand occupants of stations that fail “this tick,” but the spawner creates failures later in that tick. Following that ordering leaves the occupant seated until the next tick; implementing immediate standing inside `failStation` instead contradicts the stated ownership by `powerStep`. Specify one transition point and test a failure produced through the actual spawner and `step()`. [Step order](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-power-and-consoles-design.md:39), [forced stand](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-power-and-consoles-design.md:79), [failure helper](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-power-and-consoles-design.md:95).

4. Medium — **§2 and §4: A sitting Confirm can also activate the console.** `interactSystem` seats players before `consoleSystem` dispatches seated Confirm actions, without specifying consumption of the winning player’s edge. At Doors, the same press can therefore sit and immediately seal the first door; combined Confirm, Cancel and Interact inputs also lack precedence, including whether standing uses `interactPressed` or `interactHeld`. Define action precedence and whether newly seated players dispatch console input that tick. 5. Medium — **§3–4 and §10: Engineering’s cursor mixes station and kind indices.** Arrays and the cursor use ship-data indices, but Engineering’s displayed list uses kind order. These differ in the starter ship: Engineering is station index 6 but kind 3, while Doors is station index 1 but kind 6. Resetting the cursor to Engineering’s station index can highlight Doors in the prescribed panel unless cursor conversion and traversal are explicitly defined. 6. Medium — **§6: Remote and local door commands bypass shared arbitration.** Existing local Confirms are deduplicated to one toggle per door per tick, but the new remote command calls `toggleSeal` separately in `consoleSystem`. A local and remote Confirm on the same door therefore toggle twice, potentially cancelling a deferred seal request, despite the existing simultaneous-Confirm rule. Specify whether all door commands share deduplication or deliberately execute sequentially, and test that conflict. 7. Medium — **§9: Allocation validation admits fractional power bars.** The rules specify a number bounded by minimum, maximum and total capacity, but never require an integer. A valid allocation such as 1.5 is truncated when placed in `Uint8Array`, silently changing the authored allocation and pool accounting. Require integer allocations and include a fractional failing fixture. [Validation rules](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-power-and-consoles-design.md:131), [storage type](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-power-and-consoles-design.md:53).

8. Medium — **§4 and §10: Doors has no empty-selection behaviour.** Ship validation permits connected layouts made entirely from opening edges, so `doorEdges` can legitimately be empty. Sitting then resets to a nonexistent first door, cycling has no valid wrap range, and the required `DoorsPanel` cannot supply a door index and room pair. Either reject such layouts or define a nullable selection, disabled commands and an empty panel. 9. Medium — **§11: The inert-console hash comparison cannot establish “no effect.”** Sitting and standing legitimately change authoritative seating and stick state; the repeat counter is not required to reset on standing, so directional input at an inert console can leave a different hash. Snapping and immobilising crew can also change room breathing and oxygen history, which excluding sit/stand positions does not remove. Compare explicitly allowed state differences under controlled environmental conditions instead of requiring whole-state hash equality.
