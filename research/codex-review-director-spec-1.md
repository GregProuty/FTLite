# Codex review: 4-i spec (mechanics, 2026-11-10)

1. **Medium — [Spec L89](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-director-design.md:89): solo concurrency is not reduced.** The formula gives identical caps to 1, 2 and 3 players. Foundation §5 explicitly requires a lower solo cap; reducing budget does not reduce concurrency from spread, impacts or overheating. **Fix:** apply a solo cap reduction, clamped to at least 1, and state whether `SPAWN_CAP_MAX` also scales.

2. **Medium — [Spec L93](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-director-design.md:93): productivity is observed after conditions can disappear.** `xp.ts` observes immediately after `consoleSystem`; the proposed director runs after hazard processing. Cooling can remove the last overdrive condition, or overheating can force an operator to stand, so XP records productive operation while `sinceProductive` increments. **Fix:** capture productivity alongside `xpSystem`, including Weapons/Life Support stamp predicates, and consume that observation in the director.

3. **Medium — [Spec L92](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-director-design.md:92): breach can never receive a routing bonus.** Fire and breach always have identical Doors idle times; the earlier-type tie rule always chooses Fire, regardless of recent-type down-weighting. Foundation §5 pairs both hazards with Doors. **Fix:** select the responding-console group first and bonus both matching types, retaining deterministic tie-breaking between groups.

4. **Medium — [Spec L92](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-director-design.md:92): Life Support is omitted from routing.** Foundation §5 explicitly includes Life Support’s refill work following breach repair. Its idle counter is maintained but cannot influence selection. **Fix:** define a Life Support contribution to breach routing, including how it combines with Doors idle time and behaves when either console is absent.

5. **Medium — [Spec L75–82](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-director-design.md:75): transition versus spawn ordering is unspecified.** When stress reaches peak or the stall expires on a due-spawn tick, spawning first permits another hazard and consumes RNG; transitioning first does neither. Budget exhaustion likewise lacks an explicit same-tick transition rule. **Fix:** specify: update stress, evaluate existing exit conditions before spawning, attempt only if still in Build Up, then transition immediately if a successful spawn exhausts budget; enforce one transition per tick.

6. **Medium — [Spec L80](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-director-design.md:80): Build Up re-arming lacks a complete timing/RNG contract.** The expression adds a seconds-valued interval directly to ticks and does not explicitly say whether entry consumes a fresh jitter draw. Current spawner timing uses `Math.round(seconds * TICK_HZ * jitter)`. **Fix:** specify that exact conversion, `jitter = 1 + SPAWN_JITTER * (2*u - 1)`, and exactly one director draw on re-entry, distinct from attempt draws; initial arming remains fixed and draw-free.

7. **Medium — [Spec L55](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-director-design.md:55): initial budget omits player count.** Initialization calls `cycleBudget(0)`, whereas §7 requires `(minutes, players)`. Implementing the stated formula with an omitted argument produces `NaN`; assuming two players defeats first-cycle scaling. **Fix:** initialize with `cycleBudget(0, playerCount)`.

8. **Medium — [Spec L16 and L64](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-director-design.md:64): unattended has conflicting definitions.** §2 uses proximity/repair; §5 requires room membership for fire, adds Downed crew and excludes all debris. The debris exemption also changes foundation §5’s blanket unattended-hazard rule. **Fix:** consolidate one definition: explicitly adopt current fire repair geometry, use `inReachOfTile` for breach/failure and centre distance for revival, and document the Downed/debris policy as an explicit foundation amendment.

9. **Low — [Spec L113](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-director-design.md:113): minute-10 weight arithmetic is wrong.** Before history/routing adjustments, §7 yields `[4, 2, 6, 8]`: Power triples from 2 to 6; Debris doubles from 4 to 8. **Fix:** replace “the demanding weights have doubled” with those exact values and multipliers.
