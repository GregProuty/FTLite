2. **Important — A §2b, lines 60–63:** Interior damage can destroy the enemy, but `damageEnemy` immediately calls `leave(state, true)`, which clears enemy fields and ends the encounter ([enemy.ts](/C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/src/renderer/src/sim/enemy.ts:71), [enemy.ts](/C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/src/renderer/src/sim/enemy.ts:94)). Applying room damage after that can apply effects to an absent enemy; applying damage in multiple rooms can call `leave` again. **Fix:** Resolve interior effects while the enemy is still present, then make lethal damage/departure terminate the interior tick immediately; guard `leave` against repeated calls.
