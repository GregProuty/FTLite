# Codex review: 3-i spec (mechanics, 2026-11-10)

1. **High — [Spec line 40](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-skills-and-xp-design.md:40): impact downing occurs before `xpSystem`.** `debris.ts:76` calls `downCrew` immediately, which clears the seat and marks the operator Downed. The proposed Conscious/occupied checks therefore discard XP for earlier productive work, contradicting the stated downing-tick guarantee. **Fix:** record eligible operator IDs and productive work when each station acts; award that recorded XP even if the operator subsequently stands or goes down.

2. **Medium — [Spec line 66](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-skills-and-xp-design.md:66): post-action predicates can erase completed work.** `medbayStep` can finish healing the last injured crew member before XP checks health; shots or impacts can remove the last warned object before Navigation/Science XP checks it. Operators lose their final productive tick. **Fix:** specify observation points for each predicate and capture productivity before the relevant action removes its qualifying condition.

3. **High — [Spec line 96](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-skills-and-xp-design.md:96): shedding during Engineering failure conflicts with frozen allocations.** Foundation §5 says allocations stay unchanged while Engineering is failed. Current `failStation` sets `failed` before standing the operator, and `shiftBar` refuses all changes while Engineering is failed. With 12 allocated bars against base capacity 10, losing two bonus bars requires changing those frozen allocations. **Fix:** explicitly make automatic shedding an exception to the freeze; decrement allocations directly rather than through `shiftBar`, preserve Engineering’s reserved bar, and complete shedding before subsequent systems run.

4. **Medium — [Spec lines 80–83](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-skills-and-xp-design.md:80): unset stamps satisfy the productive windows.** With the specified `-1` initialization, Engineering and Doors qualify through tick 298 without any change, and Life Support qualifies through tick 3 without delivery. **Fix:** require `stamp >= 0 && 0 <= tick - stamp && tick - stamp < window` for all three stamp predicates.

5. **Medium — [Spec line 81](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-skills-and-xp-design.md:81): Life Support delivery has no operator attribution.** Injection can occur unmanned, then an operator can sit during the remaining hazard period and receive XP from that earlier delivery. This differs from foundation §5’s “seated and injection actually delivered … this hazard tick.” **Fix:** award `XP_RATE × HAZARD_DT` once on positive delivery to the operator seated during injection, or record that operator alongside the delivery stamp and credit only them.

6. **Medium — [Spec line 16](C:/Users/djuar/FTLite/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-skills-and-xp-design.md:16): progression contradicts the foundation tuning target.** The table correctly yields level 5 after 1,200 productive seconds, but foundation §5 targets approximately 2–3 levels for a dedicated player over 20 minutes. Navigation lump awards accelerate progression further. **Fix:** explicitly revise the foundation target, or double the thresholds to place 1,200 continuous productive seconds at level 3 and account separately for lump XP.
